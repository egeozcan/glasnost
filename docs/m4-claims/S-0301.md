Quirk switches:

- `quirks.building_ammo_fast_rearm`: on is tested in `claim_2_building_with_spare_rounds_takes_one_tick` and `claim_10_building_with_three_rounds`; off is tested in `quirk_building_ammo_fast_rearm_off`.
- `quirks.shared_wave_slot`: not implemented. Live sonic and magnetic waves are not modelled, so neither setting is observable.
- `quirks.speed_normalised_delays`: claim 1 says it does not apply. Both settings give the same delays in `claim_1_game_speed_and_speed_normalisation_do_not_change_d`.

Code fix made for this spec: claim 13. A shot made in the sync tick after the countdown is over counted as a non-sync shot and restarted the firing-frame counter (`fire.rs`). The fix is covered by `claim_13_sync_shot_does_not_restart_the_counter`.
