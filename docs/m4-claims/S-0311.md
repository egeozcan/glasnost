## Edge cases

- Untrimmed ability list items: `edge_ability_items_not_trimmed`. Revision 2 puts them under `quirks.list_items_untrimmed` (an importer switch, `ini::Quirks`): `ability_lists_follow_list_items_untrimmed` in `crates/convert/src/sim_combat_tests.rs` (on: the item keeps its whitespace and both warnings; off: trimmed; an unknown item warns in either setting).
- Bad data (negative ratio, negative cap, negative map value): `edge_negative_ratio_and_cap`, `edge_negative_map_veterancy`.
- Team-created objects: out of scope.
