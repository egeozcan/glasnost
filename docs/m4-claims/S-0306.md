## Code fixes made while writing these tests

- `claim4_flak_scatter_uses_the_range_of_the_weapon_fired`: *R*_W was always slot 0's range.
- `claim15_cruise_*`: the 18-lepton cruise lift was missing, and the look-ahead cell used P′ + trunc(6 V) instead of P′ + 6 × trunc(V).
- `t1_steering_deck_crossing*`: the steering deck row used the straight row's crossing (20 below the deck going down, and not strict).
- `t1_passed_by_*`: the passed-by row was snapped like a reached row; it is an arrival only for a jumpjet firer.

Revision 4 changed claims 3, 11, 15 and 16, F12, F13, T1 and T2. The lifted position of claim 15 and the vertical deck crossing are implemented here (request Q-0078 answered; its interim row is retired). The lead speed *u* of claim 3 and F6 (Q-0071) and the table exceptions of the F12 and F13 pitch conversions (Q-0077) rest on S-0207 claim 18 and S-0204 claim 12, which the movement session implemented (`drive::speed_estimate`, `geom::pitch_of_angle`, merged from main; requests Q-0071 and Q-0077 answered and their interim rows retired); the rows above for claim 3, F6 and F13 describe that code.

Revision 5 needs no simulation change: claim 3's *u* is named S-0207's speed estimate (claim 18, F2a), which `drive::speed_estimate` already is; F13's exception correction is stated by the sign of θ_c (+1, −1, none for zero), which `geom::pitch_of_angle` already does (scenarios P4 variant h and the P9 control); claim 16's import rows (the art section's missing, empty and no-type values, the zero-delay warning from the art delay) are the importer's (request Q-0160, now answered).
