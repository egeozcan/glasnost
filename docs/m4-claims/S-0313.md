# S-0313 claim ledger (firing positions)

Tests: `crates/deterministic/sim/tests/claims_s0313.rs`. Expected shifts are computed
independently with S-0105's table trigonometry and F2's angle in double precision, within
the 1-lepton tolerance of scenarios P1 and P3. They are checked exactly where θ is 0 or the
spec gives the numbers.

| claim | status | test names or reason |

Slope tilt in the body transform (S-0106) is not applied, and the test map is flat. |
