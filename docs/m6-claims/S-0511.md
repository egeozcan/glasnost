# S-0511 claim ledger (bridges)

Tests: `crates/deterministic/sim/tests/claims_s0511.rs` (an invented theater with both bridge
roles, 4 × 4 bridge tiles and overlays 0 to 238), the unit tests in
`crates/deterministic/sim/src/bridges/pieces.rs`, the fall tests in `claims_s0503.rs`, and the
importer tests named in the last row. Code: `crates/deterministic/sim/src/bridges/`
(`mod.rs` the attempt dispatch and area attempts, `pieces.rs` families, positions, sections and
low roles, `high.rs` sections, notices, piers, span checks and band raises, `low.rs` low strips,
`blow.rs` blow-ups, deck drops and heading hits, `hut.rs` demolition, repair and the cursor test),
`fall.rs`, with hooks in `combat::area` (claim 1), `combat::damage` (hut expiry), `targeting`
(row I3), `tick` (the fall step) and `triggers` (event 31, entered-by and destroyed-by raises).
