# S-0519 claim ledger (terror and attack animals)

Revision 2 of the spec. Tests (`crates/deterministic/sim/tests/`, fixture `terror_common`):
`claims_s0519.rs` (planting, timing, disarm, credit, visibility, the detonate order, suicide),
`claims_s0519_more.rs` (ownership, off-map carriers, the order's name, charges) and
`claims_s0519_strike.rs` (the air-strike designation); the importer tests of
`crates/convert/src/sim_terror_tests.rs`; the AI tests of `crates/deterministic/ai/tests/terror.rs`;
the lane scene `terror` of `crates/app/tests/m6c_lane.rs`.
Code: `glasnost_sim::bomb` (bombs, charges, suicide) and `glasnost_sim::strike` (strikes), hooks in
`combat::projectile` (planting, disarming and the designation call; none of the three does area damage),
`combat::check` (rank 9), `combat::fire` (the suicide shot, the second-weapon delay), `combat::death`
(a killed carrier), `combat::damage` (the charge planter as the attacker of the expiry hit),
`objects::remove` (bomb and strike leave), `structures::{ownership,entry}`, `targeting` (rows I2 and
G3, the detonate action), `orders` (`Order::Detonate`), `tick` (the carrier's and the designator's
turn), `air` (strike aircraft in retreat fall back; a spent strike aircraft retreats);
`convert::sim_combat` (warnings), state in `CombatState` and `World::strike` (save format 41).
