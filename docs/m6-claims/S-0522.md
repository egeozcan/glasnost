# S-0522 claim ledger (parachutes and drops)

Tests: `crates/deterministic/sim/tests/claims_s0522.rs` (claims 6 to 15, the drop draws of claim
16, a full drop and a save round trip mid-descent), on its own invented rules. The launch side
(claims 1 to 3 and the launch draws of claim 16) is tested in `claims_s0513_planes.rs`, and the
arrival edge (claims 4 and 5) in `claims_s0501.rs`. The parachute drawing is tested in
`crates/app/src/mapview/live_tests.rs` (`parachute_follows_its_paratrooper`), and the AI's use in
`crates/deterministic/ai/tests/ai.rs` (`fires_a_paradrop_whose_troops_join_the_army`).

Code: `crates/deterministic/sim/src/paradrop.rs` (approach, overfly, drop attempt, shot drop),
with hooks in `air` (state dispatch, pass counter, silent exit), `superweapons::planes` (sending),
`fall` (parachute recurrence, deck landing surface), `infantry` (parachute sequence),
`transport::drop_out` and `combat::fire::shot`. The app's attached animation is in
`crates/app/src/mapview/live.rs` (`attach_parachute`, `follow_owners`).
