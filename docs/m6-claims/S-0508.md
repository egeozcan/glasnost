# S-0508 claim ledger (garrisons)

Revision 5. Tests: `crates/deterministic/sim/tests/claims_s0508.rs` (entry, ownership, eviction,
assault, erasure, defeat; fixtures in `structures_common`), `claims_s0508_fire.rs` (what a
garrisoned building does: the claims of S-0308, S-0313, S-0301, S-0302, S-0307, S-0311 and S-0215
that S-0508 fills in; the map's eviction and hunt actions; Q-0970), `claims_s0214_garrison.rs` (the
cursor test of row I8), the importer tests `garrison_fields` and `occupant_animation_field`
(`crates/convert/src/sim_combat_tests.rs`), the audio tests `garrisoned_feedback_is_the_entering_units`
and `abandoned_announcement_follows_the_radar_event`, the app test `occupied_frames`
(`mapview/quirks.rs`), the AI tests in `crates/deterministic/ai/tests/special.rs` and the lane scene
`garrison` of `crates/app/tests/m6c_lane.rs`. Code: `glasnost_sim::structures` (`garrison.rs` the
occupants, `entry.rs` the arrival dispatcher, `ownership.rs` the building ownership change), with hooks in
`walk::tick` (arrival), `econ::events::turn` (the building's turn), `process` (*unload*),
`econ::sell` (sale), `combat::death` (destruction and occupants killed inside), `combat::weapon`,
`combat::fire`, `combat::damage`, `combat::range`, `combat::position` and `combat::veterancy`.
State: `Object::structure` (`StructState`: occupants, shared index, the building an occupant is in;
save format 32).
