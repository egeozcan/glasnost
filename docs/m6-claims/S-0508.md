## Not built or open

- Mind control (S-0601) and erasure (S-0514) answer no in the occupation test; a map-placed object's occupants (S-0009 claim 40) do not exist.
- Q-0970 to Q-0973 are answered by revision 7 (claims 10, 14, 22, 23, 24) and their interim rules are retired. Q-0990 (assault animation for list position 10 or later) is answered by revision 8's claim 25 (engine design rule, no quirk switch) and its interim rule is retired.
- The importer reads `MuzzleFlash`n once per place of the occupant capacity; revision 7 reads n from 0 through 9 for an assault, so with a capacity below 10 (a negative one under the equality quirk included) a position from the capacity up to 9 gets the default (0, 0) instead of a configured point. Stock capacities are small and the points beyond them hold no animation use; not changed.
- No save format bump for revision 7: no state field changed (`StructState` is as before); a save with a retained dead place from before this revision would carry a stale id in an occupants list.
- The state hash and save carry the occupants (`Object::structure`); `Game` (the AI) carries its counters.
