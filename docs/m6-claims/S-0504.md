# S-0504 claim ledger (naval units)

Tests: `crates/deterministic/sim/tests/claims_s0504.rs`, the importer tests in
`crates/convert/src/sim_naval_tests.rs`, and the wake test `claim_23_driving_wakes` in
`claims_s0207.rs`. Code: `crates/deterministic/sim/src/naval.rs`, with hooks in `drive`
(`wake_check`, `moving_now`, `progress`, the ship crush mode), `crush` (the ship crush
slowdown), `objects` (ships carry `DriveState`), `tick::object_turn` (the sinking update, the
locomotor gate; healing goes on while sinking), `combat::death`, `combat::check`,
`combat::acquire`, `combat::damage` and `combat::data` (`cloakable`).
