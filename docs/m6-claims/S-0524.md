# S-0524 claim ledger (multiplayer trigger completion)

Tests: `crates/deterministic/sim/tests/claims_s0524.rs`, on the superweapon rules of
`tests/sw_common` with an invented demolition warhead, on synthetic maps with triggers and
waypoints. The waypoint nuclear strike's launch inputs are also tested in `claims_s0513_nuke.rs`,
and the action-item and carry-over import in `crates/convert/src/map/tests.rs`
(`carry_over_flag_and_action_items`).

66, 73, 90, 108 and 130) and from `load::new_game` (the starting-building record). Codes 102 and
95 hand over to `superweapons::storm::request` and `superweapons::nuke::waypoint`.
