# S-0518 claim ledger (linked weapons)

Revision 2 of the spec. Tests (`crates/deterministic/sim/tests/`, fixture `linked_common`):
`claims_s0518.rs` (prism support, F1, the quirks) and `claims_s0518_charge.rs` (chargers, overpowered
state, weapon choice rows, charging, break-off, save); the importer tests of
`crates/convert/src/sim_linked_tests.rs` (including F4 and the layering quirk); the AI tests of
`crates/deterministic/ai/tests/linked.rs`; the lane scene `prism` of `crates/app/tests/m6c_lane.rs`.
Code: `glasnost_sim::linked`, hooks in `combat::behave` (the master's path and the resets, the delayed shot),
`combat::projectile` (the multiplier, the charge registration), `combat::check` (a pending beam answers
rearming), `combat::weapon` (table P rows 10 and 11), `process` (the charge start and the break-off),
`tick` (supporter and building turns), `objects::remove` (charger lists), `spawn::set_own_target` (the mark),
`econ::power::chargers`, `convert::layers` (F4); state in `CombatState` (save format 40).
