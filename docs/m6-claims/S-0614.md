# S-0614 claim ledger (parasites)

Revision 9 (M6c step 1e, [step1e-m6c.md](step1e-m6c.md); answers Q-1032): claim 24 is built in `parasite::carrier_ended` with the switch `quirks.parasite_suppressed_latch_strands_unit` (default on): a detonation with takeover, bomb planting or charging selected strands the carried parasite (off the map, unattached, no return, no timer, no death, experience or replacement) whatever the target; off, it attempts claim 4's return and never attaches. Tests `claim_24_a_higher_hit_strands_the_carried_parasite`, `claim_24_switch_off_returns_the_parasite_without_attaching`, `claim_24_control_selected_attachment_to_a_lost_target_returns`. The interim rule of Q-1032 is retired. Revision 8 (M6c step 1d, [step1d-m6c.md](step1d-m6c.md)): claims 1, 3 and 4 and the state table apply only when attachment is the special hit selected by S-0514 claim 20. Satisfied by `parasite::carrier_ended` (it reads `special_of`); new tests `p14_a_higher_row_suppresses_attachment` and `p14_selected_attachment_outranks_erasure` (scenario P14). The revision does not say what becomes of the carried parasite when a higher row suppresses attachment: request Q-1032 stayed open with its interim rule (answered by revision 9) (the parasite fails to latch).
