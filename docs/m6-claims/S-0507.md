# S-0507 claim ledger (transports)

Tests: `crates/deterministic/sim/tests/claims_s0507.rs` (land, air, deaths, open-topped),
`crates/deterministic/sim/tests/claims_s0507_water.rs` (hover and ship transports), the importer
tests in `crates/convert/src/sim_transport_tests.rs`, the AI test
`ferries_infantry_across_a_strait` in `crates/deterministic/ai/tests/ai.rs`, and the sim-gate
duels, which assert at least one load, one unload and a save and load while a unit rides
(`crates/sim-gate/src/lib.rs`). Code: `crates/deterministic/sim/src/transport.rs`, with hooks in
`targeting` (the enter and deploy cursors), `orders` (targets cleared and handed down), `process`
(*enter* and *unload*), `walk`, `drive` and `hover` (arrival boards), `tick` (passengers leave the
update order; open-topped sync), `objects` (leaving the map, removal), `combat::death` and `air`
(passenger fates), `combat::weapon` (passenger weapons) and `parasite::host_unloading`.
