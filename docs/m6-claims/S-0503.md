# S-0503 claim ledger (hover and amphibious movement)

Tests: `crates/deterministic/sim/tests/claims_s0503.rs`, the importer tests in
`crates/convert/src/sim_hover_tests.rs`, the bob constants test `hover_bob_constants` in
`crates/deterministic/sim/src/consts.rs`, and the AI test `a_hover_crosses_the_sea_to_a_land_goal`
in `crates/deterministic/ai/tests/ai.rs` (with the reach-region check of `Script::reachable`'s zone), and `hover_exit_reports_clear_and_factory_closes` in `claims_s0417.rs`. Code: `crates/deterministic/sim/src/hover.rs`, with hooks
in `tick::object_turn` (the locomotor arm), `objects` (the state, the placement flag and η),
`drive` (shared step-check helpers, the claims released at removal), `path` (S-0212 claim 23's
clearing, claim 8's current speed), `occupancy::in_progress`, `scatter` (claim 16 g, the scatter
centre), `infantry::destination_assigned` (claim 4's ρ = 1), `econ::release::send_out` (ρ = ½ on a
factory exit) and `econ::power::apply_disabled` (switching off).
