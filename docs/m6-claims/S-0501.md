# S-0501 Aircraft flight, attack runs and pads: claim ledger (M6a)

Code: `glasnost_sim::air`, with hooks in `tick` (dead-branch crash, pad turn, end turn, the fly
locomotor), `process` (readiness, behaviour turn, fall-back rows), `objects` (placement, taking
inside), `teleport` (removal marks), `combat::{check, death, fire, weapon}`, `econ::release`
(release docked) and `spawn` (carrier hooks, crash). Rules: `SimRules::air` (`air::AirRules`).
Import: `glasnost_convert` (aircraft flight and pad keys, commit 76db460). Tests:
`crates/deterministic/sim/tests/claims_s0501.rs` (31 tests), and S-0407 claim 16 in
`claims_s0407.rs`.

Status: **tested** (a test checks it), **partly** (built, and only some cases are tested or some
cases are seams), **seam** (an entry point exists, and the behaviour waits on another lane),
**not implemented**.
