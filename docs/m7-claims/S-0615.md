# S-0615 claim ledger (special-hit effects)

Revision 2 of the spec (built at M7b step 3.2). Tests (`crates/deterministic/sim/tests/`): `claims_s0615.rs`
(37 tests: death kinds, the burning walker, the disc laser, the quirk both ways, save and load); two pairs in
`pairs_m7b.rs` (`a_lift_fall_crushes_silently_but_its_impact_makes_a_burning_walker`,

`crates/convert/src/sim_special_tests.rs`; the audio tests `disc_laser_sounds` and `walker_and_beam_sounds`;

accepts a disc shot (`Effect::Disc` with `DiscEvent::Shot`), since a disc shot makes no `Effect::Shot`.

`glasnost_sim::walker` (claims 4 to 11: creation, the water search, the step and fallback, passability,
running, arrival, the deck fall, expiry and damage over time), `glasnost_sim::disc` (claims 12 to 18: the

projectile), `tick` (S-0203 row 12 runs `disc::update`; a walker's turn in the update order), `load`
(registration index 1, the walker and disc fields), the client (`mapview::live`: a walker's drawn
animation follows its position and F1 frame; disc beams are drawn as marks), audio (`events`: the charge-up
and report sounds of `Effect::Disc`).
