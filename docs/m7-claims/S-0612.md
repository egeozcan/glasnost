## S-0104 parts built here

The global lighting state that S-0612 times (S-0104 claims 2, 4, 5 and 14, *Nuke flash timing*, the
zero-rate and rate-scale edge cases): `s0104_ambient_step`, `s0104_dominator_fade_rate` (both positions of
`dominator_rate_thousandths`), `s0104_nuke_flash`, `s0104_set_base`, and `claim_7_zero_rate` (both positions
of `zero_rate_freezes_ambient`). The two switches left the NOT_IMPLEMENTED list. The view relights its cells
and terrain tiles when the game's current ambient or chosen set changes (`Painter::relight`): a full
recompute with the chosen set's terms and tint, which stands in for claim 5's retint and claim 9's
incremental update. Claims 10 and 16's height terms, the per-cell ranking and the static objects of the
cell pass are view seams; the chrono flash is not built.

Requests: none.

Seams filled: `superweapons::launch::yuri_seam` (removed), `mind::dominate` (called by the sweep),
`bunker::holds_through_owner_change` (doc), `psychic` and `superweapons::protect` (docs), the AI's
`fired_kind`, the doc rows of S-0513, S-0601, S-0606, S-0608, S-0611 and of the step 1 ledgers. S-0701's
announcements for the dominator are the generic launch event (`SwEvent::Launch`); no seam names the
dominator there.
