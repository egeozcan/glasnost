## Own AI (M5b)

`glasnost_ai::game::Game` runs every game with AI houses: the world plus one `brain::Brain` per AI house of the setup (`HouseSetup.ai` and the player role, glossary *AI house*). In each tick, after the person's orders, every AI house that is not defeated or removed thinks and its orders go through `orders::issue` like a person's; then `tick::step` runs. The brains are saved with the game (`Game::save`, `Game::load`) and hashed with the world (`Game::state_hash`), and a replay holds only the setup and the person's orders: `Game::run_replay` runs the AI again. The AI draws from no random stream.

The brain plays in two layers (PLAN §4.7 stage 1 and enough of stage 2):

- **The plan** (`scripted::Script`, M5a's scripted builder), every `THINK_PERIOD` ticks: deploy the MCV, build power, a refinery and factories, keep two harvesters, build the army round-robin and attack in waves (see `scripted.rs`). It rebuilds lost power, refineries, factories and harvesters, since it asks for each role whenever the house has none (fewer harvesters than the plan's).
- **Selling out** (part of the plan's think): a house whose harvesters' summed loads and credits show no income for `BROKE_PATIENCE` ticks (a rise within `SALE_WINDOW` ticks of its own sale counts as the refund) and that cannot afford anything it could build sells its costliest sellable building (`sell::sellable`, S-0408 claim 3), one per patience period, keeping one factory per kind while it has army units, and from then on builds no buildings, harvesters or construction yard, only army units. Income ends the mode. A house with nothing left to fight with thus sells out, so a game between two broke houses ends. The loads of its slaves on the map count with the harvesters', so a house whose slaves are delivering is not broke.
- **The reactions**, every tick (no reaction delay): place a finished building at once; defend the base (every army unit within 30 cells of home that is not already fighting one attacks the nearest seen enemy within 12 cells of a house building); rebuild a lost construction yard (build the cheapest buildable unit that deploys into one, queued behind what the line holds); and move an idle deploying unit that could not deploy where it stands to the nearest spiral cell where the deploy's cell test (S-0415 claim 3, the answer a person's deploy cursor gives) passes.

- **New units (M6a).** Aircraft are army of their own kind in the round-robin; a type the house cannot build yet makes the tech climb build its blocker, so a pad for an aircraft and a naval yard for a ship (ordered only when the placement spiral has a cell for it, which for a yard means water). An idle aircraft with ammunition attacks the nearest seen enemy it can hit and flies back to rearm by itself (S-0501); aircraft are kept out of the land waves. A launcher that spawns rockets (S-0506) is army like any armed vehicle. A unit whose goal lies outside its reach region (`Script::reachable`, S-0212 claim 4: a ship for a goal on land) goes to the nearest cell of its own region instead, as a person sends ships to the water off an enemy base. Defence and attacks pick only targets one of the unit's weapons can hit (`scripted::can_attack`: `weapon::would_use` gives a weapon and the firing check answers neither *illegal* nor *impossible*), so ground units do not chase hovering aircraft.

What it knows is what a person in its seat knows: its own objects, its production lines and sidebar offer, enemy objects on cells its house has explored (`econ::shroud::explored`; fog of war is off in a skirmish, S-0409 claim 19, so a person sees what stands there), the map's start waypoints (lobby knowledge), and the answers of order-legality checks a person's cursor gives (`placement::place_legal`, `placement::cell_test`). Its placement spacing (`Script::free`) counts only its own objects and those on explored cells; it keeps a gap of two cells between buildings, and `place_cell` narrows the gap to one cell and then none when the base is too cramped for any cell with the wider gap (M6c step 2d). A unit resting in guard that holds a target out of its weapon's range counts as idle (M6c step 2a, `stale_guard_target`). A refinery is a building with the refinery flag or a member of the refinery group (S-0407 Import mapping, `PROC`); a role the house cannot build a type for yet does not hold up the next one, and the army waits for harvesters only while a harvester type is buildable, or a vehicle factory is while the house has none. Refused orders are not an error: the next think tries again. Every number in `brain.rs` and `scripted.rs` is tuning of our own opponent (`nocite`), not game behaviour. Types are found by role from the rules among those the house can build, so every faction plays with its own types. A slave miner (S-0402 claim 14) plays the harvester role and its deployed building counts towards the harvesters kept; it is built without a refinery, and a resting one whose slaves are home is sent to harvest with the nearest explored resource cell as its destination, the order a person gives it (no other order starts a player house's slave miner); it then finds its site and deploys by S-0402 claim 22. A slave with a master is never army, even when its type is armed. Only a vehicle that deploys into a construction yard is deployed as the MCV or kept as the lost yard's replacement.

## Own AI (M6c: special abilities)

`glasnost_ai::special` (one module of its own, hooked into `Brain::think` once per think period) uses the special abilities the way a person in the house's seat could: it clicks (`targeting::cursor` and `targeting::click`, so the cursor rules of S-0214 decide what a click means) on objects of cells its house has explored (`econ::shroud::explored`) and issues the resulting orders through `orders::issue`. It reads no hidden state and draws no random value.
