# S-0406 claim ledger (building placement)

Revision 19 (sim issues S35, #440; Q-3243 answered with M-0027). Claim 17's factory break-off: the place order requests the named building's *idle* body as a deferred change (`placement::break_off`), which takes effect at the end of its update on its first turn *T* + 1 (`placement::break_off_turn_end`). Until its first construction processing *C* it is in the factory break-off window (`Construction::break_off`, `placement::in_break_off_window`): out of the construction state, so its first per-tick start is not withheld, still not constructed; the idle range sets the mark, and *construction* still queued becomes current at the first start of that tick and is processed in it. With a zero-delay idle range *C* = *T* + 2 and the build-up runs once to *H* = *C* + (*n* − 1) × *d_b* (F6, F7); a zero-delay construction range sets the mark on *T* + 1 and is current from its second start, *C* = *T* + 2 too. *C* resets stage 0 and ends the window. A deployed building, a filling building and a restored replacement get no break-off; the last two keep the two runs while `quirks.buildup_twice_without_break_off` (renamed from `quirks.placed_buildup_twice`) is on, and get the break-off with it off. A stage advance is made only in an update and the next delay counts from it (S-0108 Terms, r111; `Construction::stage`, `since`), so a missed turn holds a due advance. The current construction runs its range whatever started it (claims 14 and 15; `placement::construction_turn`). In the window, the refinery's acceptance and the stop and deploy refusals apply only while construction is current (Changes for other specs; `placement::refused_as_in_construction`). An owner change in the window with construction current and not processed is Q-3261 (Open questions item 10); `claims_s0309_r126.rs` ends the window by hand for that row. A deploy accepted in the window queues *unload*.
