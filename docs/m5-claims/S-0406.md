# S-0406 claim ledger (building placement)

Revisions 7 and 8. A refused placement now requests the placement refusal announcement, not the game-wide refusal sound: at the click (`play::Play::click_map`, `UiCue::PlacementRefused`) and when the order is applied and fails its cell tests or claim 12's upgrade-target recheck (`placement::refused_cue`; `claim_1_place_order_through_production`); a missing factory stays silent. Claim 10's retention of a damaged wall is now an explicit interim rule of our engine (already so). Claim 12's application-time target recheck was already so (`upgrade_target` at application). Claim 20 (Q-0282): the click adds no battlefield text (the "Cannot place there." message is removed; `select_move_deploy_build_and_place_through_the_client_model`). Claim 19 (Q-0277, the placement display's frames 0, 2 and 3 and the arrow during placement) is not brought over: the viewer still draws its own preview. Revision 6. Claim 12 now follows S-0404 claim 10 (an upgrade order needs no adjacency but explored ground; `placement::place_legal`), and claim 11 and the Edge cases say `quirks.single_stage_buildup_never_completes` also governs selling (S-0408 claim 17, done in `econ::sell` at revision 8). No code change. Revision 5. Claim 1: a factory building queued to be sold cannot make either (`placement::factory_could_make`, `claim_1_factory_queued_to_be_sold_cannot_make`). Claim 16 (Q-0220) adds the deployed building's turret facing 0 (already so) and that a deploy facing outside 0 … 255 never deploys (`claim_6_out_of_range_deploy_facing_never_deploys` in `claims_s0415.rs`; the converter warns, `deploy_facing_out_of_range_warning`). Claim 12's upgrade-order legality (no adjacency, explored ground) was already `place_legal`'s. Revision 4. Claims 17 and 18, F7 and two Edge-cases switches are new (Q-0130 answered): *construction* starts only at the ready mark, which a deployed building has on entering play and a placed one gets once its build-up range has run (`quirks.placed_buildup_twice`), and a one-stage range with a positive delay never completes (`quirks.single_stage_buildup_never_completes`; the converter imports the build-up frame count and warns about such types). Revision 3. Claims 14 to 16 and F6 are new (construction cadence and handover, placed facing; Q-0133's placement half). Revision 2. Claim 3 (b) now leaves listing to S-0209 claim 13 (a landed aircraft is listed and refuses, an aircraft in ordinary flight is not listed and does not), and claim 3 (d) names the bridge-body mark of S-0010 claim 25. No code change: the object test walks the cell's listed objects (claim 3 b, so a landed aircraft refuses) and the ground test reads the deck and bridge-body marks (`bridge_marked`). Ordinary flight cannot happen yet (aircraft stand landed; flight is a seam), so the not-blocking half of claim 3 (b) has no test.
