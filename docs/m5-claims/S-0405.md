# S-0405 claim ledger (power)

Revision 11. The needs-engineer row of claim 8 now reads the *manned* state (Terms; S-0515 claim 2), not physical engineer presence: `BuildingSwitches::manned` is set by `power::owner_changed` at a building's first ownership change of any cause and never cleared; placement does not man a building. It is saved and state-hashed (save format version 16). Claim 6's spy blackout comes only through S-0510's single-effect selection (doc comment of `power::start_blackout`; no code change). Revision 8. Claim 19 and F4 are new (Q-0276's power-bar part): the settled meter's base *B* (`power::meter_base`) and its occupied capacity and three extents (`power::meter`); the sidebar draws them (`glasnost_ui::hud::power_meter`). The quantisation of the extents into drawn bands is our layout's. Revision 7. Wording of claim 18's reference to S-0009 claim 40 only; no code change. Revision 6. Claims 1 and 2 now state that with a positive upgrade count every recorded position contributes, positions above the count included (Q-0224; already so, `held_upgrades` reads all three positions), with scenario W12 part (g) (`w12_g_positions_above_the_count_contribute`). Claim 18 points to S-0009 claim 40 (no code change). Revision 5. Claims 1 and 2 and F1 and F2 now state that recorded upgrades count only while the upgrade count is positive (Q-0224 answered; the gate was already the interim rule), and scenario W12 gains part (f), the turret upgrade sale (`w12_f_turret_upgrade_sale`). Revision 4. Claim 18 (Q-0180) is new; claim 7 now states that surrender and defeat destruction comes after the house update's power evaluation (already so: `econ::houses` runs power before `victory::house_update`).
