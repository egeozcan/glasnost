# S-0417 claim ledger (factory unloading)

Revision 9. Claim 13's refinery boundary (Q-0371): apron clearing is the vehicle factory's and tests E only; a refinery without the vehicle factory flag requests nothing of a unit on its docking cell (`claim_13_refinery_boundary`; `release::idle_apron` already checked the flag). Revisions 5 to 8. The short-list apron pair now follows the original list's length *L* (10: (32767, 32767); below 10: (0, 0)) rather than naming words; the results for S-0404's words are unchanged (`edge_short_list_words`). Claim 10's clear report is the first standing-position arrival after the tether (S-0208 claim 23 answers Q-0295; the interim row is retired; `walk` already reports on the arrival tick). Claim 2 notes that an armed factory can get the ready mark from its resting processing (S-0215 claim 32, owned by the sim-core session; not checked here). The rest are paired-revision notes and scenario detail. Revision 4. The Edge cases decide Q-0221 (the short-list alternative, the illegal apron and the full-ring independence) with the interim rules already written; their interim row is retired. Revision 3. Claim 7 settles which updates of a driving vehicle are its cell-arrival updates (Q-0223: reaching a track target, also when a further track follows in the same tick; the chain-point update needs curve chaining, which is not written). Claim 2's closing and unloading wait for the ready mark (Q-0222, `quirks.factory_unload_waits_ready_mark`). Claim 10 gives no destination without a rally point. Claim 14 is new: contact places, the infantry exit-search entry test and the edge-entering aircraft's nearby destination (the rest of Q-0120). What a walking infantry unit's cell-arrival update is stays open (request Q-0295; interim: the arrival tick). Revision 2. Claim 10 now gives the infantry unit the rally point as its guard spot before it is put in play, a first leg to its exit cell and a second to the rally (a jumpjet or teleporting type goes straight); claim 11 gives a docked aircraft contact and a tether; claim 13 (idle apron clearing by a factory in guard) is new, and claim 12 counts its draws. Revision 1. S-0417 replaces the interim unloading and way-out rule of request Q-0120. Q-0120 stays open only for the parts this spec leaves open (infantry docking place and walk-out, the exit-search entry test, the aircraft's docking position and the cell near the factory).
