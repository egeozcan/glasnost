# S-0417 claim ledger (factory unloading)

Revision 2. Claim 10 now gives the infantry unit the rally point as its guard spot before it is put in play, a first leg to its exit cell and a second to the rally (a jumpjet or teleporting type goes straight); claim 11 gives a docked aircraft contact and a tether; claim 13 (idle apron clearing by a factory in guard) is new, and claim 12 counts its draws. Revision 1. S-0417 replaces the interim unloading and way-out rule of request Q-0120. Q-0120 stays open only for the parts this spec leaves open (infantry docking place and walk-out, the exit-search entry test, the aircraft's docking position and the cell near the factory).

Tests: `crates/deterministic/sim/tests/claims_s0417.rs`, and `claim_13_way_out` in `claims_s0404.rs`. Code: `crates/deterministic/sim/src/econ/release.rs` (release, apron, door, the unloading stages), with `drive.rs` (exit track T1, speed fraction, the cell-arrival report), `walk.rs` (infantry report), `tick.rs` (temporary driving locomotion), `process.rs` (the unloading dispatch and the hand-back of a replaced locomotor) and `orders.rs` (orders while tethered).
