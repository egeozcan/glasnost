# S-0417 claim ledger (factory unloading)

Revision 20 (taken in at M6c step 1g, [../m6-claims/step1g-m6c.md](../m6-claims/step1g-m6c.md); answers Q-1110 question 3). Claim 16 is new: the lifetime of temporary driving after a factory exit (the exit point is retained without a timer, a later rally, scatter or ore destination replaces it in the driving locomotor, its completion or a stop clears it, a later eligible fall-back or a qualified docking assignment restores the teleporting locomotor). It was already as built; tests `claims_s0417_r20.rs`. Claim 5 now says that a qualified docking assignment that itself restores supplies its point to the restored locomotion (S-0207 claim 27; `claims_s0207_r34.rs`). Revisions 17 to 19 (taken in at M6c step 1f, [../m6-claims/step1f-m6c.md](../m6-claims/step1f-m6c.md); answer Q-1070). Claim 15 is new: a released jumpjet infantry unit or vehicle ends factory contact and tether in its ascent clear report (`econ::release::ascent_report`; tests `claims_s0417_r19.rs`, `claim_15_*`), claim 7 notes that this report is not a cell-arrival update, claim 10 and Open question 9 point at it. Claim 5's restoration text: a temporarily driving vehicle gets its own locomotor back only with a suspended predecessor, no driving movement report (no locomotor destination point and no heading point horizontally different from the actual position), no restoration lock and no seizure (`econ::release::restorable`, `claim_5_restoration_needs_no_movement_report`). Revision 16. Claim 5 (Q-0793, S-0207 claims 24 and 25): the driving exit track stores heading height 0, and the successful exit instruction supplies the locomotor destination point at the apron centre at height 0 (`drive::begin_exit_track`). Claim 3 is reworded: the vehicle's own height follows the movement rules. Revision 9. Claim 13's refinery boundary (Q-0371): apron clearing is the vehicle factory's and tests E only; a refinery without the vehicle factory flag requests nothing of a unit on its docking cell (`claim_13_refinery_boundary`; `release::idle_apron` already checked the flag). Revisions 5 to 8. The short-list apron pair now follows the original list's length *L* (10: (32767, 32767); below 10: (0, 0)) rather than naming words; the results for S-0404's words are unchanged (`edge_short_list_words`). Claim 10's clear report is the first standing-position arrival after the tether (S-0208 claim 23 answers Q-0295; the interim row is retired; `walk` already reports on the arrival tick). Claim 2 notes that an armed factory can get the ready mark from its resting processing (S-0215 claim 32, owned by the sim-core session; not checked here). The rest are paired-revision notes and scenario detail. Revision 4. The Edge cases decide Q-0221 (the short-list alternative, the illegal apron and the full-ring independence) with the interim rules already written; their interim row is retired. Revision 3. Claim 7 settles which updates of a driving vehicle are its cell-arrival updates (Q-0223: reaching a track target, also when a further track follows in the same tick; the chain-point update needs curve chaining, which is not written). Claim 2's closing and unloading wait for the ready mark (Q-0222, `quirks.factory_unload_waits_ready_mark`). Claim 10 gives no destination without a rally point. Claim 14 is new: contact places, the infantry exit-search entry test and the edge-entering aircraft's nearby destination (the rest of Q-0120). What a walking infantry unit's cell-arrival update is stays open (request Q-0295; interim: the arrival tick). Revision 2. Claim 10 now gives the infantry unit the rally point as its guard spot before it is put in play, a first leg to its exit cell and a second to the rally (a jumpjet or teleporting type goes straight); claim 11 gives a docked aircraft contact and a tether; claim 13 (idle apron clearing by a factory in guard) is new, and claim 12 counts its draws. Revision 1. S-0417 replaces the interim unloading and way-out rule of request Q-0120. Q-0120 stays open only for the parts this spec leaves open (infantry docking place and walk-out, the exit-search entry test, the aircraft's docking position and the cell near the factory).

Tests: `crates/deterministic/sim/tests/claims_s0417.rs`, and `claim_13_way_out` in `claims_s0404.rs`. Code: `crates/deterministic/sim/src/econ/release.rs` (release, apron, door, the unloading stages), with `drive.rs` (exit track T1, speed fraction, the cell-arrival report), `walk.rs` (infantry report), `tick.rs` (temporary driving locomotion), `process.rs` (the unloading dispatch and the hand-back of a replaced locomotor) and `orders.rs` (orders while tethered).
