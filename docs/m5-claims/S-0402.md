# S-0402 claim ledger (harvest cycle)

Revisions 9 to 12. Claims 19 to 25 (Q-0270) are new: initial slaves from creation, created not factory-built, group updates and emergence, harvest directing the miner, ownership on a change of house, the deployment site and the relocation boundary; claim 14 is restated around them. None is implemented: our engine creates no slave objects yet (claim 14 stays a seam), so these are open. `AIVirtualPurifiers` is read as a decimal-prefix list (S-0003, 82245fa). Q-0371 asks what happens to a harvester at rest on a docking cell while another holds the dock. Revision 8. Claim 10's second route (Q-0197) is implemented: `harvest::track_arrival`, called from `drive::spend` at a reached track target after the position and the cleared track and before cell-centre crushing; a refusal by a refinery in *selling* sends a forced vehicle scatter request without a threat point and not move-anywhere (`scatter::request`, S-0207 claim 16), as the sold-refinery scatters of claim 11 now do too. Claim 13's failed step-off (Q-0194) sets destination (0, 0) under the new `quirks.harvester_failed_step_off_origin` (off: clears it). Both interim rows are retired. Revision 7. Claim 17 now defers the teleport to S-0207 claim 20, under which a harvester does not phase in after a jump (already so: `claim_20_harvester_does_not_phase_in` in `claims_s0207_teleport.rs`); the slave wake rule is restated in words and the square-root switch gets its off state (no code change). Revision 6.
