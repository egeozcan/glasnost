# S-0402 claim ledger (harvest cycle)

Revision 33 (taken in after M7b, [step1g-m7b.md](../m7-claims/step1g-m7b.md)): claim 19's creation numbers and order within a creation set (S-0611 claim 14 and F5). The slave group is now filled after the spawn slots (it came first before), and an infantry or aircraft type that names a slave type now gets its group at creation (`harvest::Rules::group_of`; before, only vehicles and buildings did). Scenario H15 points to S-0611 MU12.

Revision 28 (taken in at M6c step 1h, [step1h-m6c.md](../m6-claims/step1h-m6c.md); with S-0207 revision 36 it answers Q-1130): the seeking drop of claim 17 is built. A harvester whose active locomotor teleports drops its destination, an adopted remembered cell included, at every seeking processing past the *full* test. It then runs the ring search from where it stands (`harvest::seeking`). The landing point fixed for the dropped destination keeps its reservation until the next accepted destination (S-0207 claim 20 h). Tests: `claims_s0402_r28.rs`. Revision 27 (taken in at M6c step 1g, [step1g-m6c.md](../m6-claims/step1g-m6c.md); with S-0207 revision 34 and S-0417 revision 20 it answers Q-1110): a chrono miner travels by its active locomotion. A factory-built miner drives its first leg to the ore while still temporarily driving (claim 17, *Temporary driving after production*; `claims_s0417_r20.rs`). On a qualified restoration in a docking-cell assignment, the restored teleporting locomotor takes that same offer (claim 10; `claims_s0207_r34.rs`). The seeking drop of claim 17 stays unbuilt: request Q-1130 asks which seeking processings drop the destination. Revision 26 (step 1f, [step1f-m6c.md](../m6-claims/step1f-m6c.md)): claim 10 row 2's repeated offers (`claim_10_row_2_offers_reach_the_dock_test`).

Revision 25 (taken in at M6c step 1b, [step1b-m6c.md](../m6-claims/step1b-m6c.md)): claim 3b and F1a (Q-0900 answered) are satisfied by the existing seeking rules (`harvest::seeking` tests the order-level destination; due behaviour processing runs before locomotion); test `claim_3b_loading_starts_after_the_destination_is_removed` in `claims_s0402.rs`.
