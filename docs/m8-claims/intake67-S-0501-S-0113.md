# S-0501 r65 and S-0113 r17: claim ledger, M8 step A3 intake 67

S-0501 r63 to r65 (commit be0579e) answers **Q-2237**, how the damage smoke is made. S-0113 r16
to r17 (commit e33bc50) answers **Q-2236**, how a crashing aircraft's body is drawn with its
tumble. Both questions came from A4 round 7 (play note 40,
[a4-play-notes.md](a4-play-notes.md)), which built interims that this intake replaces. No
request was filed.

## Built

- **The damage-smoke animation** (S-0501 claim 37, Q-2237). `air::smoke` now creates the
  animation itself when a smoke is shown.
  - It calls `anims::spawn` with the fixed type `air::DAMAGE_SMOKE`, found without regard to
    letter case (Import mapping), at `o.pos`. That is the position when *u* is taken, after the
    aircraft's own movement in `air::end_turn`.
  - The animation has no owner and is not attached. It has loop delay 0, repeat factor 1 and no
    reverse request.
  - The creation's draws (S-0109 claim 3's delay draw, then claim 13's start event) come
    through `anims::spawn`, right after *u*.
  - It pushes `Effect::Anim` so the view draws it.
  - With no type of that name, nothing is created and *u* is still taken.
  - With `quirks.aircraft_smoke_scenario_draw` off, neither the draw nor the animation happens.
  - No new state is added: the smoke is an ordinary animation in `w.anims`.
- **A crashing aircraft's body** (S-0113 claim 20, Q-2236).
  - `voxel::crashing_body_pose` draws *V* · Rz(*θ*<sub>flight</sub>) · Rx(*φ*) · Ry(*ψ*′) with
    no lift, shift or tilt (`voxel::crashing_body_local`). The shadow is *V* · Rz(heading).
  - `voxel::crash_pitch` gives *ψ*′. When the speed fraction is strictly above the type's
    drawing-pitch threshold, it adds the drawing-pitch angle in double precision, without
    narrowing the angle first, and stores the sum in single precision, truncated toward zero
    (`to_f32_rz(add_rz(..))`). Otherwise *ψ*′ is *ψ*.
  - `mapview::build::tilt` gives `Tilt::Tumbling { pitch: ψ′, roll }` only at strictly positive
    height. At zero or negative height it gives level flight's heading-only body. The bank
    never enters.
  - `live::attitude_with` takes the tumble's exact angles from `Rocking::exact_angles`, which are
    not held to the 0.005 drawing step. The drawn transform depends on *ψ*′ and *φ* only
    through their F1 table steps, so the body image's cache key is those two steps. A kept
    image is then pixel for pixel a fresh draw, as for a rocket's fresh pitch (S-0105
    claim 41), and the images kept are bounded by the table (in the lane, one per tick of a
    fall). The shadow keeps level flight's key.

**Where the answers differ from round 7's interims:**

| Interim (round 7) | Answer (intake 67) |

| The pitch was the tumble pitch alone | *ψ*′ adds the drawing-pitch angle under S-0105 claim 42's speed test, with the sum at 53-bit precision |
| The tumble was drawn at every height while crashing | Only at strictly positive height; at zero or negative height the heading alone |
| The tumble angles were held to the 0.005 drawing step (the rocking cache) | Exact angles; the body image is keyed on their F1 table steps, which fix the drawn transform |
| `Effect::CrashTumble` and `mapview::rocking::crash_rates` (the tumble's rates and draws) | Unchanged: S-0312 claim 16's draws were already the answer, and claim 20 adds only the drawing |
| The damage smoke was presentation only: the draw was simulated, the animation was not | The sim creates a `SGRYSMK1` animation through `anims::spawn`, with its creation draws on the scenario stream |
