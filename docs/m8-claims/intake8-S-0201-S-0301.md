network game, and Q-1770, a delayed building shot against the water and land rows.

**Code.**

- **The delayed building shot.**
  - `combat::check::check`: a building with a pending delayed shot answers *rearming* first, unless
    `econ::power::firing_answer` (S-0405 table T1) answers.
  - The magnetic-beam rearming stays at rank 5 on its own. The Q-1770 interim (the rows first) is
    retired.
  - `combat::behave`: starting the delay stores the chosen slot in `delayed_slot`.
    `delayed_shot_turn` checks that slot, with range, against the target as it then stands, and
    fires only on *can fire*. Otherwise nothing is made.
  - `linked::start_shot` resets the slot.
- **The speed order** (`orders::Order::GameSpeed`).
  - It is applied with the house's other orders and sets `World::game_speed`. An index above 6 is
    ignored.
  - It is not refused by the order block of the first ticks.
  - `Play::change_speed` gives it: straight to the game in a skirmish, through the lockstep
    outbox in a network game.
- **The options menu** (`game::session`).
  - `open_options` is refused once `victory::ended`, and after a surrender through the menu until
    `victory::defeated` (`menu_allowed`).
  - The speed control is offered while the local house is not defeated (`speed_offered`). The
    menu holds its own `menu_speed`, and `close_options` sends the order if it differs from the
    index in force: the game's in a network game, the paced one in a skirmish.
  - In a network game the menu's title does not say paused and it offers no settings screen
    (`hud::pause_view`).
  - No battlefield redraw while the menu is open or the window lacks the focus in a network game;
    closing the menu redraws.
  - After a network tick the paced index follows `World::game_speed`.
- **Network pacing.**
  - `glasnost_ui::setup::net_period_ms`: ⌊1000 / cap⌋ with the cap [60, 45, 30, 20, 15, 12, 10],
    which gives [16, 22, 33, 50, 66, 83, 100] ms.
  - It replaces `period_ms` in `client_net` (pacer, lockstep config, grace and stall periods), in
    the relay's rejoin grace (`hub.rs`), in the bot (`bot.rs`) and in the soak's synthetic room
    (index 0, still 16 ms).
  - `Session::set_tick_ms` follows a carried-out speed order.
  - The pacer gains `due_after` and `remaining_for` with an explicit period.
  - `net_tick` names the house of every other slot whose speed order was taken: "NAME changed
    the game speed.".
- **The active clock** (`client::update`): active whenever a network game is, and in a skirmish
  while the menu is closed (lost focus counts).

## Seams

- A skirmish's `World::game_speed` changes at the end of the next tick, through the order path,
  while the paced period changes at the menu's close.
- The relay's rejoin grace and the lockstep's resend interval stay at the start's period after a
  speed change.
- The render-layer row of S-0306 claim 23 is not modelled.
- The client clock counts no freeze; our client loop has no freeze.
- A network game's unfocused window adds no wait (claim 16, our engine's departure). The host
  may still present the last frame.
- The speed notice names the house by its player's name.
- The pending-order limit (S-0213 claim 2) also counts speed orders.
- None of the original's 30-tick start, tick-rate negotiation or 256-tick schedule (S-0201
  claim 17's description of the original) is built: our lockstep keeps its own delay.
