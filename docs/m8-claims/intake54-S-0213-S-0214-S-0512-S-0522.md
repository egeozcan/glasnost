## What changes

- **Route points (S-0213 claims 16a to 16f).**
  - Point adds share a point only when they are applied in the same tick. A later add makes a
    new point, even at an existing point's place (16a).
  - Being committed belongs to each route, not to the place. A later add at a committed point's
    place adds a new, uncommitted point, and the actor waits there for the next commit (16c).
  - A join copies the route of the point's longest holder. A tie in one tick goes to the order
    applied first (16b). The copy keeps a loop that starts at or after the joined point. A loop
    that starts before it is rotated, so the copy is the whole loop entered at the joined point.
    The copied points are uncommitted.
  - Giving up at the commit is a queue of *wait* with an immediate start, and so is each issue
    at a fall-back to rest. While *wait* is current, an opportunity target is dropped and no
    shot is made at it (16c).
  - Issuing a loop point moves it to the end of the route (16c).
  - A point that no route holds is deleted at once, whichever way its last holder left it (16f).
- **The falling firer (S-0214 Terms and claim 6 (e); S-0522 claim 17).** The firing rules refuse
  every target to a falling firer. So during the fall, an ordinary click on an enemy gives
  table M's move order. A force-fire click gives an attack order at once, carried out after
  landing.
- **Held type-select drags (S-0214 claim 32).** With the role held, every live box candidate,
  of any house or kind, stands for its own type. The drag adds the own objects of those types
  that can be selected within the reach. It adds none of a tap's deploys-into types. Claim 19's
  clearing and empty-box rules still apply.
- **Icon sounds (S-0512 claim 25).** Every left click on a superweapon icon first requests the
  build click sound, whatever the icon's state. The arming input then requests the
  select-target announcement ([EVA_SelectTarget]). A no-aim icon requests the click only.

## Built

- **`glasnost_sim::routes`**:
  - `RoutePoint.made` is the tick of the add, so only same-tick adds share a point.
  - `Step.committed` and `Step.since` are per-step state. `since` is stamped from a new
    `RouteWorld.stamp`, and the join takes the smallest stamp.
  - `Route.next` is gone: the next step is the first step. A loop issue moves the step to the
    end, and a plain issue removes it and lowers `loop_from`.
  - `wait` queues *wait* with an immediate start (S-0215 claim 7). It runs before the issue at
    the commit and at each fall-back.
  - `drop_orphans` runs after every issue.
  - `point_for` gives Q-2166's interim pick.
- **Falling**: `combat::check::check_ignoring_fall` is gone. Claim 6 (e) asks
  `combat::check::check` again, falling refusal included, and force-fire bypasses it.
- **Client**: `Play::type_drag` (`play.rs`) is the held drag. The session's drag branch calls
  it while the type-select role is held, with the reach's pool.
- **Audio**: `UiCue::SuperweaponClick` (the build click) and `UiCue::SelectTarget`, with
  `Announcement::SelectTarget` mapped to `EVA_SelectTarget` (S-0512 Import mapping).
  `Play::cameo_click` pushes the click first, then the announcement when it arms.
- Save format 88 to 89 (the route stamp and per-step state). AI game format 50 to 51. Wire
  version unchanged: no order kind changed.
