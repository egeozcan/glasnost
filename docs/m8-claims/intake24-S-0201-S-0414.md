# S-0201 r31 and S-0414 r30: claim ledger, M8 step A3 intake 24

This intake takes in two spec revisions. Neither answers an open request of ours, so no interim
is retired. Q-0193 stays answered in part only (S-0414 Changes for other specs), and its interim
in `econ/production.rs` stays.

- **S-0201 revision 31** (spec commit 0d6ad85; diff against 00e6720, revision 23, which intake 8
  built). The changes in substance:
  - Claim 1: in a skirmish the options menu offers one way to leave. It ends the game at once,
    and no choice resigns (S-0703 claim 13). The old open question (which choice is which) is
    gone.
  - Claims 8 and 17, the lead's ruling of 2026-10-24: in a skirmish watched on after the human's
    defeat, the menu shows the speed control, and a change takes effect as in claim 8. In a
    network game the control is still hidden after the defeat.
  - Claim 15: the refusal of the menu after a surrender through it is now a network game's only.
    New paragraph *What leaving offers in a network game*: while at least two houses of human
    participants are undefeated, the player's own among them, and the player is not an
    observer, two choices send the surrender order (one then leaves, one keeps the player
    watching). Otherwise there is only a leave, which sends no surrender order.
  - Scenario T1 part D: one leave choice in a skirmish. T8 part D: also the leave choices
    offered. T8 part F: the two choices, a further leave-only run, a three-machine run.
  - Questions for the project lead are now a *Decided* list (2026-10-25). The earlier Changes for
    other specs (to S-0414) are gone, since S-0414 took them.
  - The rest is confidence, backing and wording ("this revision" becomes "this spec").
- **S-0414 revision 30** (spec commit 2eed3bf; diff against f9b7495, revision 16, which intake 8
  built). The changes in substance:
  - Claim 21: a hit with an attacking house and no attacking object is credited to that house as
    a kill. A not-scored victim gives no kill, but by such a hit its house still takes a loss. An
    insignificant building gives neither, even when also not scored. A hit whose attacking house
    differs from the attacking object's owner is credited to the house; the object's owner gains
    no kill. A destruction that is not a hit counts for the house of the object S-0311 claim 9
    credits. A destruction with neither an attacking object nor an attacking house gives no kill.
  - Claim 22: two new rows score the attacking house: price × the victim's rank, 0 when the house
    allies the victim's house (as the scoring house sees its alliances), with no not-scored or
    insignificant exclusion. The object row now covers every destruction credited to an object,
    including those that are not hits.
  - Summary, claim 11 and the Measurement scenarios: a skirmish's menu sends no surrender, so
    every run of the original with the human's surrender is given in a new *network-game form*.
    Ratings resting only on such a run are lowered to low (claims 5, 11, 15 to 18, 21, 22).
  - Scenario V17 gets parts s (attacking-house score, with gas particles) and t (an erasure's
    score). V4, V8, V11, V12, V14 and V15 surrender in the network-game form; V15's ticks become
    8*m*.
  - Changes for other specs: S-0311 claim 7, S-0407 claim 12, S-0508/S-0313/S-0308, S-0311 claim
    15, S-0601 claim 14, Q-0193 (answered in part).

**The lead's ruling** (S-0201: "the game-speed control stays shown after the player's defeat").
r31 states it for a skirmish (claims 8 and 17, citing the ruling of 2026-10-24). For a network
game r31 keeps the control hidden after the defeat (claim 17, scenario T8 part D). Our code
follows r31: shown in a skirmish after the defeat, hidden in a network game. "The player's defeat"
in the ruling is read as the skirmish case, the only one in which our engine lets the person watch
on after the human's defeat with the original showing nothing.

**Code.**
- `sim/src/econ/stats.rs`: `destruction(w, victim, credit: Credit)`, with `Credit::{Nobody,
  Object(id), House(h)}` and `Credit::object(Option<ObjectId>)`. The loss goes to the victim's
  house when the victim is significant and scored, or when the credit is a house. The kill goes
  to the scoring house when the victim is significant and scored. The score goes when the victim
  is scored or the credit is a house.
- `sim/src/combat/damage.rs` `credit`: maps S-0302 claim 14's (object, house) pair to the credit.
  Experience (`veterancy::destruction_credited`) is unchanged.
- `sim/src/econ/slaves.rs`: a slave destroyed inside its master is credited to the killing hit's
  attacker object only (S-0312 claim 30, a destruction that is not a hit); a removed or sold
  master's slaves to nobody.
- `ui/src/hud.rs`: `PauseHit::SurrenderLeave`; `pause_view` takes the leave choices to show
  ("Surrender", "Surrender and leave", "Leave game").
- `app/src/game/session.rs`: `leave_choices`, `speed_offered` (`!network || !defeated`),
  `menu_allowed` (the resign refusal for a network game only), `leave_after_surrender`.
- `app/src/game/client_net.rs` `net_tick`: after a tick that carried out the local slot's own
  `Order::Surrender`, a client with `leave_after_surrender` leaves.

**Tests.**
- `sim/tests/claims_s0414.rs`: `claims_21_and_22_a_kill_credited_to_the_attacking_house` (new:
  rookie, not-scored, insignificant, insignificant and not-scored, allied victims, and a hit
  whose house differs from the attacker's owner); `claim_21_kills_and_losses` (its last case is
  now neither an attacking object nor an attacking house).
- `sim/tests/claims_s0613.rs`: `a_warhead_cloud_kill_is_credited_to_its_house` (new: a
  warhead-created cloud's particle kill scores its house; the shared system's gives nothing).
- `ui/src/hud.rs`: `options_menu_offers_the_leave_choices_it_is_given` (new);
  `options_menu_without_the_speed_control` (now network only).
- `app/src/game/client_access_tests.rs`:
  `the_menu_is_refused_after_the_end_and_offers_the_speed_after_the_defeat` (replaces
  `the_menu_is_refused_after_the_end_and_after_a_surrender`);
  `a_network_menu_offers_the_surrenders_while_two_humans_stand` (new).
- `app/src/game/client_net_tests.rs`: `a_client_that_went_reconnects_and_plays_on_in_lockstep`
  now ends with Ann's *Surrender and leave* over the relay. Her client leaves, and both others
  hold her house marked to die by the tick she left (well before the drop's grace).
