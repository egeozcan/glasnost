## What changed

### The crush response (S-0215 claim 39, S-0210 claim 11)

S-0210 claim 11 had an "automatic crushing choice" with no stated occasion, which we never built
(Q-2005; [intake 38](intake38-M-0011.md), [step 2 ledger](../m6-claims/step2-movement.md) claim 11
row). r29 and r88 replace it with the **crush response**: a reaction to a hit, the only occasion.

- **Occasion.** A hit on a vehicle that has been applied and has not killed it: every result of
  S-0302 claim 12 but *now dead* and *post-mortem*, so *unaffected* and *unchanged* count, and
  berserk hits too. The hit must name an attacker (S-0302 claim 1). No behaviour's processing or
  search makes the choice; an enemy that does not hit the crusher is never chosen.
- **Conditions.** The vehicle is not tethered and not a team member (no house of our engine has
  teams); its owner is not allied with the attacker's owner (a house is allied with itself); the
  owner is allowed by S-0210 claim 11's table; the vehicle is a crusher; the attacker is crushable
  by it (claim 2) and does not appear disguised to its house (S-0516 claims 35 and 36); and the
  3-D centre distance (the approximate root of S-0208 F8) is at most the crush range (F2, `≤`).
- **The owner.** The original's computer-controlled row: skill level at least `[IQ] AutoCrush`
  (absent: 2) and difficulty index not 2. In our engine every player house, AI included, counts as
  human-controlled (glossary *AI house*), so by default no player crusher responds. The neutral
  and special houses and test-only computer-controlled player houses keep the original's row
  whatever the switch. With `quirks.auto_crush_computer_only` off, a player house's crusher
  responds when `PlayerAutoCrush` is on or its type has `AutoCrush`, with no skill, difficulty or
  handicap condition.
- **Effect.** The destination becomes the attacker object, under the rules of every new
  destination (S-0607 claim 9, S-0517 claim 9, S-0606 claim 18, S-0207 claim 20 (a)). *move* is
  queued, with no immediate start, only when the destination is accepted. The target is not
  changed. A later qualifying hit replaces the destination.
- **Order within the hit.** The response follows every other effect of the hit: the berserk
  entry's queued *hunt* is replaced by *move*; on a retaliation the attack override comes first
  and *move* is queued behind it.

### S-0215 claim 37 part c (r88, Q-2113)

The old exception "the target is the object that holds the actor with a magnetic beam" is gone. A
seized unit whose target is its own lifter now takes the approach step as for any target; a
destination the step gives it has no effect (S-0607 claim 9). The other half of part c, a lifter
gripping a unit whose target is that unit, is the rule intake 49 built from S-0607 r18 claim 11;
r88 states it in S-0215 too.

### Other S-0215 r88 changes

Wording that drops stock-data remarks (claims 6, 11, 32, F9, Edge cases, Open questions); claim 6
(temporary driving and the restorations of S-0207 claim 27 and claim 13 are not locomotor
replacements that force a behaviour); claim 11's B44 note (a move click while falling gives an
order at once, S-0522 claim 17); claim 13's magnetic-lift row deferring to S-0607 claim 20;
scenario B33 (e) and B44's variant b; claim 37's backing and confidence; new Open questions
(claim 39 and a later revert; *move* after a refused destination); Cross-spec changes (S-0402's
harvester return, S-0309 and S-0610 on M-0011's held target); Changes for other specs (S-0302
claims 10 and 12, S-0610 claim 4); Questions for the project lead.

### S-0216 r31

Claims 1 to 5 are reworded with no change of rule. Claim 6 is rewritten: no unit of the records'
games was placed on the five cliff cells (M-0006). Claim 7 is new: facts about the records' units
(placed cells from M-0006), not a rule of the game.

  above, each cited.
- `sim/src/combat/damage.rs`: `damage::apply` now wraps the hit rules (`apply_hit`, the old body)
  and calls the response after them for every result but *now dead* and *post-mortem*. Every hit
  goes through `damage::apply`, so the response sits in the real tick path (area detonations carry
  `attacker: det.source`).

  assigning, so that *move* is queued only for an accepted destination.
- `sim/src/approach.rs`: the lifter-of exception removed; the gripping lifter's check re-cited to
  S-0215 claim 37 (c) r88 and S-0607 claim 11.

## Play-fixes

C7's admission rule asks that a switch's off position be stated and built, that its on position
read as a bug in play, and that it be no balance change. `quirks.auto_crush_computer_only` meets
the first only. Its on position is the original's rule and no stuck state, glitch or play note
names it: with stock data no player crusher makes the crush response, which a player sees as
"crushers do not chase infantry that shoot them", an ordinary rule rather than a bug. Its off
position changes behaviour whenever a data set gives `PlayerAutoCrush` or `AutoCrush`, so it is a
rule choice that touches balance. **Not added** to `data/layers/play-fixes.jsonc`.
