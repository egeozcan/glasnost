**Code.**

- `combat::check::targeting_mode_illegal`: the water and land rows. The firing check asks them
  after every rank-4 answer that S-0306 claim 23 puts first (the gripped unit, deactivated,
  hidden target, absent weapon, building power, materialising, held temporal target, held
  spawner, empty spawner, launching) and before the seized-firer and magnetic-beam answers of
  `lift::firing_answer`. The other weapon's live effect, which claim 23 also puts first but the
  check asked after `lift::firing_answer`, is asked inside the row's branch, so its *impossible*
  still wins. A target counts as airborne by height (S-0307 Terms), on a bridge by the deck layer,
  and on water or beach by the land type of its cell. Our engine has no overlay targets.
- `combat::acquire::retaliate`: F7's sight *S* of a building actor was 0 (the lookup read unit
  types only); it is now the building type's `Sight`, as S-0215's Import mapping gives. Before
  this, a building of a human-controlled owner never retaliated against an attacker it could not
  reach, whatever its sight.
- `combat::death`: row 3 takes the imported list as it is (entry *w*, else entry 0); the interim
  `no_type` filter is gone. The corpse comment cites S-0107 claim 17 and S-0615 claim 2.
- Importer: `DeathAnims` is read as a list of animation names (`Refs`), like `DeadBodies`: no-type
  and empty items are left out, unknown names keep their place and create animation types
  (S-0004 claim 13, already in `refs.rs`). A list of only no-type items is an empty list that
  replaces an earlier layer's; an empty value keeps it (S-0003 claim 12).
- `LandTargeting` and `NavalTargeting` in the importer's combat fields now also cite S-0306.

Tests: `crates/deterministic/sim/tests/claims_s0306_modes.rs` (synthetic types: anti-air-only and
anti-ground-only weapons, an underwater type, an anti-air-only building with and without the SAM
flag); `claims_s0615.rs` and the importer tests in `crates/convert/src/sim_tests.rs` for S-0107.
With the two rows switched off, 11 of the 14 new tests fail; the remaining three check rules that

## Local lane (stock data, counts only)

Scene `aa-only` of `crates/app/tests/m7b_lane.rs`, out of CI: each building type whose weapons'
projectiles are anti-air and not anti-ground, needing no power in the lane's copy of the rules,
of a human-controlled owner and of a test-only computer-controlled owner, attacked from 6 cells
by an enemy vehicle and an enemy infantry unit, and in another place by an enemy aircraft; 600
ticks each. Output in `local-lane/m8/a3-5/lane-before.txt` and `lane-after.txt` (git-ignored).

Both such building types have land-targeting mode 1, no SAM flag, a naval-targeting mode that
gives "none" on water, and the retaliate flag, so they are S-0309 claim 18's first case. Before
this step each of them, for both owners, took the infantry attacker as its target (about 90 to
104 ticks) and fired at it (2 shots each but one run, which shows only rearm restarts) until it
died. After it, neither holds or fires at either ground attacker in any run, and the infantry
survives. The vehicle attacker was not taken as a target in either run. The aircraft runs show
nothing either way: the aircraft is gone within about 10 ticks of being taken as a target, in
both runs.
