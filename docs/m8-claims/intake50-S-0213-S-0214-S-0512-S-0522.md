## What changes

- **Route mode (S-0213 claims 16 to 16g, 17, 22, table T2, Constants).** While the route-mode
  key is held, a battlefield click gives no order: it adds a route point standing for the
  behaviour order the same click would give outside route mode (16b), to the route of each
  selected object (16a; one point shared by every route that holds that place). A click on an
  existing point joins it (an empty route copies the rest of a holder's route) or closes a loop
  (16b, 16c). Releasing the key commits every point; a holder in guard or a continual behaviour
  starts at once, any other holder at its next fall-back to rest (16c, S-0215 claim 13's route
  row). A route is used up at its last point; a loop cycles. Only vehicles and infantry take
  part, the selection must be in one route state, and the house's route-holder count plus the
  new objects may not pass 128 (16, 16d). A click on a point with nothing selected selects it;
  the delete key removes the selected point from every route (16e). Holding ends when a route
  is used up, ended by an order (claim 17), emptied by deletion or its holder leaves play; points
  no route holds are deleted (16f). The guard, stop, scatter and deploy keys are refused in
  route mode, each with its own message (T2).
- **Type-select tap (S-0214 claim 32, Terms, Constants).** A press and release of the
  type-select role at most 500 ms apart is a tap. It adds the selection's types (with the
  building each deploys into) from the screen's candidates, or across the map when the screen
  has no new one or the reach is already *map*; the reach resets to *screen* after an addition or
  a clearing. Held clicks and drags read the reach and do not change it. A single foreign object
  in the selection is removed first.
- **Control groups (S-0214 claim 33, F9, Constants; S-0213 claim 22).** Ten groups, digits 1 to
  9 and 0. *Make group* (Ctrl), *add group to selection* (Shift), *centre group* (Alt) and
  *select group* (the digit); a select less than 800 ms after the last one that selected the
  group centres the view on F9's group centre point.
- **Arming from the icon (S-0512 claim 25).** An icon with an aim action empties the selection in
  the same input and arms the type; the input ends placement, sell, repair and power mode. An
  icon with no aim action fires at once and changes nothing else.
- **Orders during a fall (S-0522 claim 17; S-0214 Terms, claim 6 (e)).** Falling is no longer
  part of *movable* or *able to fire*: a move, attack or deploy click during the fall gives its
  order at once, carried out after landing.

## Built

- **`glasnost_sim::routes`** (new): `RouteWorld` (the house's points, each holder's steps and
  loop, the per-house route-holder count, the selected point), saved and hashed. `click` makes
  the click-time refusals (kinds, same route state, the holder limit, the point-click test) and
  turns the click into `Order::RoutePoint`, `RouteJoin` or nothing; `apply_point`, `apply_join`,
  `commit`, `delete` apply the orders at the order boundary; `order_ends` ends a route for every
  other order naming an actor (claim 17), except `Order::Routed`, the order a route issues for
  its point; `fall_back` is S-0215 claim 13's route row in `process.rs`; `drop_holder` ends
  holding when a holder leaves play. Messages go out as `Effect::Route` (`RouteNote`).
- **Orders**: `RoutePoint`, `RouteJoin`, `RouteCommit`, `RouteDelete` and `Routed(Box<Order>)`;
  `orders::blocked` steps past the route orders (they issue nothing to the actor).
- **Client** (`session.rs`, `client.rs`, `play.rs`, `window.rs`): `Action::RouteMode` (Z by
  default, rebindable) held is route mode; clicks go through `routes::click`; release or focus
  loss commits; `Key::Delete` deletes the selected point; the guard, stop, scatter and deploy keys
  are refused with their messages; routes and points are drawn while the key is held.
- **Type select**: `targeting::tap_types` and `tap_candidate` (with the unit-powering building),
  `PlayState::type_tap` with `Reach` and `sel_reset`; the session times the tap
  (`TYPE_SELECT_TAP_MS`, 500).
- **Control groups**: `PlayState.groups` (`BTreeMap<ObjectId, GroupMark>`, the group number and
  the house that made it), `make_group`, `select_group`, `add_group`, `centre_group`, `group(g)`;
  `targeting::group_centre` (F9); the session's `group_key` with `GROUP_REPEAT_MS` (800); the
  client maps Ctrl, Shift and Alt with a digit (Ctrl, or the held make-group key on the web).
- **Falling**: `targeting::movable` and `able_to_fire` no longer test falling; claim 6 (e) asks
  `combat::check::check_ignoring_fall`, the firing check with S-0301 claim 9's falling refusal
  left out (Q-2140 question 3). *Intake 54:* S-0214 r64 answers this the other way. Claim 6
  (e) asks `combat::check::check` again, and `check_ignoring_fall` is gone.
- Save format 87 to 88 (route state; A7 had taken 87), AI game format 49 to 50, wire version 9 to 10 (the route
  order kinds). The schema and the synthetic ruleset take the four quirks.

## C7 judgement

None of the four quirks goes into `data/layers/play-fixes.jsonc`:

- `spent_route_blocks_join`: the on position stops an object from joining points once its route
  is spent; it can still add points and take every order. No unit is stuck, no play note or
  request names it: condition 2 fails.
- `route_holder_count_drift`: only moves when the 128 limit refuses a click; no stuck unit, no
  play note: condition 2 fails.
- `group_center_keeps_extra_selection`: an interface choice (which objects *centre group* keeps),
  not a bug: condition 2 fails.
- `group_kept_on_owner_change`: an interface choice (whether a group follows an object through an
  owner change), not a bug: condition 2 fails.
