## What changed

### S-0407 claim 16 *At issue* (r29, Q-2172)

The local player's interface, and in our engine every build order, makes claim 16's test when an
order to start or queue an item is issued, with the line's copies as they are then (none for an
idle line). For a dock-bound aircraft type that is the house-wide pad capacity *D* ≥ *T*, and an
aircraft in flight is on the map, so it counts in *D* like one on its pad. A house whose listed
aircraft are all out cannot order another while their number is *T* or more. A click so refused
gives no order and plays no sound. Our engine also refuses at issue a dock-bound type at its own
build limit (claim 13's design rule with no switch); the original's interface gives that order and
S-0404 claim 3 refuses it when applied.

Built as `econ::tech::refused_at_issue` (claim 9's *at limit*, or `queue_refused` with the line's
copies), used by the sidebar click (`econ::sidebar::click`, S-0411 claim 4 row 7) and by order
legality (`econ::sidebar::legal`, S-0407 claim 13). `queue_refused` keeps
`quirks.dock_bound_aircraft_ignores_limit`, so the switch's off position also applies at issue, as
r29's switch text now says. A resume of a held item stays exempt (checked first).

### S-0307 claim 19 and S-0215 claim 37 (a1) (r20 and r91, Q-2171)

Round 6 (A4 note 37) read the *attacker's* region for its deck (`regions::same_region_read`) and
left the candidate on its ground. Claim 19 now reads a high-bridge **candidate** for its deck
(S-0212 claim 4) too, so deck cells over water can qualify, and a deck over other ground is read
for its deck whatever the ground's region. `combat::range::approach_search` passes the candidate's
deck flag; `same_region_read` gains the deck-to-deck case in which both reads give no region
(`LoneDeck`), which counts as one region. A candidate of no reach region still never matches
(claim 19 is unchanged there).

S-0215 claim 37's per-tick rule (*cannot reach its target's cell*) is now S-0212 claim 6's
comparison (part a1): `approach::reaches` reads the actor's point for its deck when it stands on
one and the target's cell for its deck whenever that cell carries a high bridge, whatever the
target's layer, and counts two cells of no reach region (or two off-map cells) as one region
(`regions::compare`, which the path gate of `path.rs` now also uses). Claim 6's exceptions skip
the comparison: an actor's cell outside the usable area but legal, and a train or *retreat* actor
inside the usable area with a legal target cell outside it. The actor's point is its locomotor's
point while it moves (`approach::a1_cell`: an infantry unit's scatter reference point,
`scatter::infantry_centre_cell`; a vehicle's heading point, `scatter::vehicle_centre_cell`) and
its own position otherwise.

### S-0607 r33 (Q-2151)

- **Claim 5.** A seized unit cannot be selected: a click, a band box and a control group's command
  pass it over (`targeting::selectable_by` and `box_eligible` add `!lift::seized`), during the
  grip, the fall and until restoration. A unit seized with a stand-in that is never restored stays
  unselectable. It is still the object under a click (`clickable_by` and `selectable` are
  unchanged; S-0214 Terms, *Can be selected*, is a hand-off of r33's *Changes for other specs*).
- **Claim 9.** Only an *effective* clear (the unit has a destination) is the no-destination
  exception that ends a grip. Part e's clearing in the approach step removes the destination
  directly, which is no assignment: the grip holds whatever the stand-in, and a later rule that
  gives the unit no destination again is redundant and changes nothing. Built by
  `lift::destination_removed`, called from the approach step's part e: it sets the lift's
  `grip_without_dest` flag, which `lift::steer` now honours by keeping the destination at none
  while the grip holds (part f's cell is ignored). `given_no_destination` no longer clears the
  flag. Before this revision the flag marked only claim 23's no-cell case; an old save cannot hold
  a grip from part e, so no format bump is needed. A balloon-hover type's S-0502 claim 28
  retention is tested before the seized unit's refusals: the grip holds and the destination is
  kept when the rejection applies.
- **Claim 13.** A drop always runs the jumpjet stop (its direct instruction); a set-down runs it
  only through an effective clear (`lift::set_down_released` reads the flag: a unit that part e
  left with no destination takes no stop and keeps its point and commitment). A balloon-hover
  set-down whose clear S-0502 claim 28 rejects keeps its destination, point and commitment, with
  *attack* current. A dropped balloon-hover vehicle still ends with no destination and the stop,
  with *attack* current (`lift::drop` asks `jump::clear_rejected` while the target is held, then
  clears).
- **Claims 9 and 20.** The seize and restoration keep an attack-move memory (nothing removed it;
  now tested). S-0518 claim 14's charging scan runs for a seized unit in *guard* (no exception in
  `linked::start_charging`; tested).

### S-0408 r22

Claim 7: a building whose type lacks the crewed flag makes no survivor attempts (*C* = 0 by S-0312
F5); the no-crew state never holds at a sale. Claim 8: the passengers' release ignores the crewed
flag. The code already did both (`structures::absorb::sold` has no crew test; `survivors::count`
reads the crewed flag): a test now pins them.
