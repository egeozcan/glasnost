# S-0519 r17, S-0215 r76 and four more: claim ledger, M8 step A3 intake 10

This intake takes in six spec revisions. One of them answers a request: Q-1740, the height that the
slot-animation shroud test uses on a sloped cell.

- **S-0519 revision 17** (spec commit cabc575). It takes in S-0501's entry for S-0519.
  - Claim 7: a carrier destroyed without a hit (S-0501 claim 46's fall-back crash) keeps its bomb
    through the fall, where the timer and the detonate order can still set it off. A bomb still
    attached at impact is removed with no effect.
  - Claim 5: a bomb set off during that fall makes one detonation.
  - The state table splits the carrier's death into a killing hit, a destruction without a hit
    and that carrier's impact.
- **S-0513 revision 34** (3e898b5; Q-1740 answered by 4a9111d). Claim 29b: the slot-animation
  shroud test is made at the terrain's surface height at the cell's centre. On a sloped cell that
  is the slope's height there. New scenario SW10 part i6.
- **S-0203 revision 40** (c4a12bc). Claim 5: a projectile whose first turn a removal costs takes
  that turn in the first later tick in which no removal costs it, not always tick *n* + 1.
- **S-0306 revisions 35 to 38** (38e767f; intake 8 built r34). These follow S-0318: a fragment is
  no shot.
  - Claims 1, 2, 3, 5, 6, 7 and 8 except fragments: speed *S*_w and no cap, no aim point,
    the burst point, the fixed elevation.
  - The note on claim 20 (c): the burst point is not moved.
  - State and ordering: *n* + 1 or later.
  - Import mapping: the shrapnel fields are S-0318's.
  - Open questions: a steering or vertical fragment's start speed, and a fragment's scatter.
- **S-0202 revisions 34 to 36** (2e3b3cd). Claim 10's bouncing row covers S-0109's sprite
  animations only. A voxel-model debris piece's creation draws are S-0317's.
- **S-0215 revision 76** (1f11ab7). Claim 32: a non-SAM building whose target leaves range
  returns to *guard* at the first processing at which the firing check answers *out of range*.
  While the check answers *rearming* (the countdown, a pending delayed shot, the other rank-5
  causes) it keeps its target. Claim 37 (g) and scenarios B16 case e and B36 part b are scoped
  to match.

No new quirk switch is named. The one S-0519 r17 names, `quirks.crash_drops_planted_bomb`, is
S-0501's and already exists. Both coverage tests are green.

**Code.** No simulation logic changed: the code already did what each revision states. This
intake adds tests and citations, and retires the Q-1740 interim:
- `combat::behave::building_process` already acts on the firing check's answer. *Rearming* comes
  ahead of the range test (S-0301 claim 9, ranks 4 and 5 against rank 10), and buildings take no
  approach step (`approach.rs`, claim 37 (g)). Revision 76 is cited at the rows of the answer
  table.
- `combat::shrapnel::fragment` already follows S-0318 claims 5 and 6, which S-0306 now defers to:
  - the speed is the weapon speed `WeaponRules::speed`, which is *S*_w after the ρ = 0 arc-speed
    replacement in `combat::data`;
  - there is no cap and no start speed 1;
  - the fragment is placed at the burst point and climbs at the fixed elevation;
  - the burst point is the point before claim 20 (c) (`projectile::detonation_point`).

  The S-0306 claims are now cited there.
- `update::UpdateOrder` applies S-0203 claim 10 in every tick, so a projectile can lose its first
  turn twice.
- `pieces::create` and `load.rs`'s bouncing draws cite S-0202 claim 10's scope.
- `mapview::live::slot_cell_shrouded`: its interim is now the claim. Comment, interim table row and
  the *Slot animations* paragraph of `docs/development.md` are updated.

New hashed state: none. Formats: save 73, AI game 34, wire 8, all unchanged; goldens unchanged.
