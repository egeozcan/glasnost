## Built

S-0213 r84:

- **The order's place** (claim 16b). `routes::order_place` gives the place, and
  `Order::RoutePoint.place` carries it:
  - an order with a target has the target's place;
  - a *move* has the clicked cell, or the cell of the clicked object at the click, even when the
    nearby-cell search moved the destination;
  - any other order has its destination;
  - an order with neither has no place (`None`), and applying it adds nothing.

  `routes::point_cell` reads the marker's cell from the place.
- **The recorded order** (claim 16b, and Changes for other specs, S-0215 claim 13). `routes::issue_next`
  sends the `Step` as it was recorded, or as a join copied it, or as the attack-move change changed
  it. It is not classified again: S-0215 claim 13's service guards and cell fallback no longer apply
  to route points.
- **Attack moves in a loop** (claims 16c and 6). `routes::loop_attack_moves` runs from
  `apply_join`'s loop close, at every close, a second close in the same tick included. Eligibility
  is read from the holders of the loop's first point (`orders::attack_move_eligible`). The change
  goes per point and per holder, over every route of the house, from the loop's first point to the
  route's end. A building holder gets claim 6's building result, *attack move* (processed as
  *sleep*).
- **A loop that ends early** (claims 16b, 16c and 16e, Q-2191). `Route.loop_to` became
  `Route.loop_len`, with `Route::loop_ends_early`. Once `loop_from` is 0, every issued point goes to
  the route's end. `loop_len` counts only in `apply_delete` (a delete at or after the loop's first
  point lowers it, and below 3 the loop is cancelled) and in `join_copy`.
- **Aircraft and buildings** (claim 16d, Q-2192).
  - `routes::fall_back` issues only for infantry and vehicles.
  - `routes::issue_later` queues *wait*, then issues. It is called from `air::landing_trigger`, at
    a flight's end unless the behaviour is *enter*.
  - It is also called from `combat::behave::building_process`, in the no-target branch of *attack*,
    for buildings without the SAM flag. When *wait* is then current, no *guard* is queued.
  - A building's rally-point click on a cell (`GuardSpot`) is sent directly at the release.
  - r87: a building's *move* on an object (`GuardSpot`, around the object's cell for a factory of
    units, naming the object otherwise) and its *deploy*'s primary order (`Primary`) are sent
    directly at the release; a building's deploy is not refused in route mode, so
    `targeting::in_route_mode`, which the client's cursor applies, keeps a building's deploy on
    itself instead of turning it into no-deploy. An absorbing
    building's *deploy* (`Order::Deploy` outside route mode) becomes a point add of *unload*
    naming the building itself, at its own place, issued as claim 16d says.
- **Q-2193.** `routes::press_kind_refusal` counts the click areas of every house's points.
  `quirks.hidden_point_refuses_click` off leaves out the points that are not drawn. A right press is
  never tested for kind.
- **Formats.** Save format 92 (`Step` and `Route` changed), AI game format 54, wire version 12
  (`Order::RoutePoint.place`).

S-0301 r63 (commit 890f3db):

- `emitters::fire_place_let_go` is the fire place's let-go (a retarget to a target not within
  reach, a move order, the firer's death). It marks whatever system the place holds, whatever its
  kind. `gas::system_turn` calls it for systems that are not fire-kind. `particles::fire_turn` lets
  go only when the fire place holds the system.
- The spark and railgun places are freed only when their system leaves play. A fire-kind system
  held there is not marked by its own turn while its owner lives.
- At creation, the fire and spark places pass the shot's target object, and the railgun place
  passes none: the place decides, not the kind.
