## Built

- **`depot::Link::arrived`** (new hashed state). Set by `depot::movement_arrival` (claim 17's
  movement arrival in the docking-point cell) and kept by `take` for a repeated request on the same
  depot. `depot::depot_destroyed` reads it (claim 22): before the arrival, the target is dropped and
  the vehicle falls back to *guard*, tethered or not; after it, the vehicle keeps its behaviour,
  *sleep* or *guard*, docked or not. With `depot_loss_leaves_sleep` off, every arrived contact gets
  the sale's run-away notice (it was every tethered one).
- **The full-health refusal** (claims 16 and 17). `depot::repeated_request` refuses a vehicle at
  full health (`health_full`, a ratio of exactly 1) after the permission test. The first
  acceptance (`order_entered`) still makes no damage test. The refusal's consequences are the
  existing ones of S-0215 claim 29: an untethered vehicle loses its contact and falls back
  (`fall_back`), a tethered one keeps it. Claim 20's release of a docked vehicle at full health by
  the order was already built (claim 23's full-health case); its tests now assert it as r107
  states it.
- **The waiting row with no contact** (claims 6 and 22). `depot::process_repair`'s waiting phase
  with no contact now starts the slot changes as before (`depot_anim::service_ended`: *special-3*
  and *active-1*), puts the depot into a new phase `LEAVING` (`nocite`: our own number) and sets the
  next processing 1 tick later. That processing clears the serviced slot and starts *guard*. Before
  this intake the depot took *guard* at once, in the processing that found no contact. The
  settling row with no contact is unchanged.
- **The client's slot play and M-0014** (`mapview::slot_play`). `Slots::prepare` now applies the
  re-enablings that come before the slots' turns (`early_enablings`): a trigger's ownership change
  of a needs-engineer type, and a pulse's end. `Slots::update` applies those after them
  (`enablings`): an ownership change in the turn pass (a capture), a pulse's start and the power
  switch. `State::captured_in_turn` tells the two kinds of ownership change apart; `Live` fills it
  from the tick's `Effect::Captured` with an announcement (S-0509 claim 12). Before this intake
  every change acted after the slots' turns. A caller that does not prepare the tick gets both
  sets in `update`, the early one first.
- **S-0601 F4 r20** needed no code: the client's overload pulse (`mapview::rocking`) already
  replaced the roll rate with an unscaled value. A test now pushes a body first.
- **S-0614 r21** needed no code: claims 9, 10 and 13 were built in intake 53 through S-0113.
- **Formats.** `Link::arrived` is saved and hashed: the save format goes 89 → 90 and the AI game
  format 51 → 52.

## Design choices

| question | our choice |
|---|---|
| Where a capture's ownership change acts against the tick's slot advances (S-0108 claim 13: in the turn pass, in update order) | After them, so the first advance comes in the next tick, as M-0014's timeline shows (**Q-2186**, `// design: Q-2186` in `Slots::enablings`). A change with no announcement (a trigger's) acts before them |
| What tells a capture from a trigger's change in the client | The announcement of S-0509 claim 12 (`Effect::Captured { announce: true }`); a change without it is taken as one before the turn pass |
| Where the depot's *guard* of the waiting row with no contact is held | A fourth repair phase, `LEAVING`, that only the next processing reads; the depot's behaviour stays *repair* for that tick |
| A replacement contact during the `LEAVING` tick (S-0314 Open questions) | Not looked at: the next processing goes to *guard* whatever it finds |
| When a vehicle becomes settled (S-0314 Terms and claim 21 leave the arrival or the first waiting processing open) | At the arrival, as before |
| The driving vehicle's behaviour after arrival (claim 17: M-0007 shows *guard*; for others open) | Unchanged: the arrival queues *sleep* for every kind |
