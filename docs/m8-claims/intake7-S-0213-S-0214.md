# S-0213 r46 and S-0214 r49 claim ledger, M8 step A3 intake 7

Two spec revisions, answering requests filed from the lead's play notes 1 and 2
([a4-play-notes.md](a4-play-notes.md)):

- S-0213 revision 46 (spec commit fa13bdb; Q-1750 answered by 5e4ea6b, Q-1753 by f92e0e2):
  claim 7 (group destinations) states what were our engine's interim rules as the original's,
  adds the entry-check inputs of the *obstructed* class, the open class for cells that units
  occupy, how often destinations are shared, and the switch `quirks.group_spread_no_detour`;
  table T2's deploy rule gains *Deployed, for the deploy rule* (members part-way through a
  transition) and Scenario O6 part B; Scenario O10 gains part f.
- S-0214 revision 49 (49f973d; Q-1754 answered by bb2c191): Terms, *deploy readiness*, names the
  timer of an infantry type with an undeploy delay (the rearm countdown) and counts *deploy* as
  a deploy kind; a vehicle's readiness does not depend on its transition state; Edge cases gain
  *infantry deploy readiness and the rearm countdown*; Scenario T24.

One new quirk switch, `group_spread_no_detour` (default on, the original; not consulted, since
the search its off position would make is not specified: S-0213 Questions for the project lead).
No new hashed state: the save format (71) and the AI game format (32) do not move. The sim-gate
goldens pass unchanged.

**Code.**

- `orders::spread` (claim 7). The leader is placed first: every member standing in the leader's
  cell gets the common cell through it and has no look (before, it ran a look with the zero
  direction, which tested the common cell six times, our interim; since the common cell is now
  among the given cells, that look would find it taken and fall back to it, so the outcome is the
  same and no test can tell the two apart). The entry check of the
  *obstructed* class takes the common cell's height level, plus 4 when the common cell is a
  high-bridge cell, as its reference level (before, the member's own reference level); our entry
  outcomes have no connecting-step restriction, so the "no connecting-step direction" input needs
  no change. The look of 6 points for each member, the first one cell out, the last-taken-cell
  fallback with no further search, the destination priority with its run-order tie-break and the
  shared-cell rule were built already as interims and now cite the claim.
- `targeting::deploy_command` (the deploy rule). A new `press_counts_deployed` sorts every
  selected in-place deployer at the press: infantry by whether its sequence is a deploy kind
  (*deploy* included, *undeploy* not), a vehicle by deploying or deployed against undeploying or
  mobile. The sorting runs before the other conditions, so a member that then fails one still
  makes the selection mixed. The other uses of "deployed" (rows I20 to I22, IC6, table M part 1)
  keep `deployed_ipd`, which S-0214's Terms define and r49 did not change.
- `targeting::deploy_readiness` (S-0214 Terms). An infantry type with an undeploy delay of 0 or
  more needs a sequence that is no deploy kind and not *undeploy*, and its rearm countdown
  (`combat.rearm_until`) to have ended. A negative delay: ready in every sequence, whatever the
  countdown. Vehicles: unchanged (no transition state was read).
- Table VD row VD2 (`targeting::vehicle_deploy`, built at A4 round 2) checked against r49: it
  asks `deploy_readiness`, whose vehicle clause r49 leaves as it was (it now says outright that
  the transition state does not count). No change.
- The own AI: `deployers` orders only infantry with a negative undeploy delay to deploy, whose
  readiness r49 does not change; no AI change.

## Local lane

None: no stock data is needed. The lead can try both rules in the native build: select a group of
vehicles on open ground and move it; the group keeps its shape around the clicked cell, and members
share a cell only next to water, cliffs, buildings or enemy units, or 7 or more cells out along a
row. For the deploy press: deploy one in-place infantry unit of a type with a negative undeploy
delay, select it with an undeployed one and press deploy while the first is still deploying: both
get the order, and the first goes back once its deploy ends.
