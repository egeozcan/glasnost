# S-0703 r51 and S-0701 r27: claim ledger, M8 step A3 intake 12

Superseded in part: the options-menu hold rows and the lost-focus seam are replaced by
[intake 25](intake25-S-0701-S-0703.md) (S-0701 r35, S-0703 r58): the music plays on in the menu
and lost focus holds it.

This intake takes in two audio spec revisions. Music is presentation: nothing here reads or
changes simulation state, and the state hash is the same with and without music
(`music::tests::state_hash_with_music`).

- **S-0703 revision 51** (spec commit 1fdad4b, from revision 34 built at C1 part 1). It answers
  Q-1831, Q-1832 and Q-1833, and Q-1830 in part (d9035e4, 24729a0, 9c4beba, 20da944).
  - New claims:
    - claim 8a: files that are present but do not play;
    - claim 11: the start of music in a skirmish, with the loading track, the map theme and the
      in-game music setting;
    - claim 12: the end of the game and the score screen, with the score track and the menu
      track;
    - claim 13: the options menu, leaving and restarting.
  - Terms: failed start, loading, score and menu tracks, map theme, in-game music setting, end of
    the game. The ended track and music started are restated.
  - Claims 1a, 2, 3, 4, 5, 7, 9 and 10 now point at claims 8a and 11.
  - Two new departures with no switch, both waiting on the lead (Edge cases): the music is held in
    the options menu, and the end screen opens when the game is over.
  - Import mapping: `LOADING`, `SCORE`, `INTRO`, `InGameMusic`, the settings' defaults and
    `[Basic]` `Theme`.
  - Scenarios: MU1 part m, MU8 to MU11.
  - Changes for other specs.
- **S-0701 revision 27** (542db92). Claim 30 changes:
  - each audio pass ends with one music update;
  - no skirmish audio pass runs after the cycle that runs the end delay's final tick;
  - the options-menu stand-still has no music update.

  H2 gains its music controls.

No quirk switch is named. `quirks.music_fallback_entry_zero` is unchanged. No simulation code
changed: `git diff main -- crates/deterministic crates/sim-gate` is empty. Formats and goldens are
unchanged.

**Code.**
- `glasnost_audio::music`:
  - `Music` has phases (`Phase::Off`, `Loading`, `Armed`, `Started`, `Score`, `Menu`), and
    `playable` gives claim 8a's rule.
  - Direct starts of the loading, theme, score and menu tracks make no draw.
  - `MusicSettings` reads `InGameMusic`. Missing or unrecognised values keep the defaults.
- `glasnost_audio::events::Audio`:
  - `set_theme`, `loading_music`, `game_over_music`, `main_menu_music`;
  - `hold_music` keeps an offset on the music's host-time clock;
  - `menu_frame` times the end screen's score track by the output.
- `glasnost_audio::mixer`: `hold_music`, which leaves the music voice out of the mix and does not
  advance it.
- `music_files`:
  - `VfsTracks::warnings` gives the claim 8a importer warning;
  - `InstallMusic.warnings`, logged by `game::install`.
- `mapview::load`: `Loaded::theme`, read from `[Basic]` `Theme`.
- `game::client`:
  - `loading_music` runs at both skirmish and network starts;
  - `screen_music` handles the screens other than the game;
  - the options-menu hold;
  - `game_over_music` once the game is over;
  - `main_menu_music` on leaving.
- Tests: `crates/audio/src/music_tests.rs` (MU1 m, MU8 to MU11) and the new
  `crates/app/src/game/client_music_tests.rs` (the same rules through the windowless client).

The interims citing Q-1830 to Q-1833 are retired from the code and from `docs/development.md`.
`c1-S-0703.md` keeps its interim list as a record of C1 part 1, with a note that the interims are
retired.

- Natively the loading track is not heard: the start job blocks the frame loop until the game is
  loaded. On the web it plays while the game loads.
- S-0701 claim 30's logical audio clock is still not built, so passes run on host time. The
  stand-still holds only the music; sounds play on as before.
- Lost focus does not hold the music (claim 30 names it as a stand-still).
- The menu track plays once. How it goes on, and what the other menus play, are open (Q-1700
  question 7).

  playable.
- No restart is offered. The three-player surrender run is not made.
- No lane run for MU8 to MU11 against the original.
