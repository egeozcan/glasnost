# S-0501 r40, S-0318 r13 and S-0503 r16 claim ledger, M8 step A3 intake 9

Three spec revisions. The first answers the aircraft requests from the lead's play note 5
([a4-play-notes.md](a4-play-notes.md)):

- **S-0501 revision 40** (spec commit 626a48b, against revision 19). It answers Q-1755 in part
  (75beeca), Q-1756 (c75bec9), Q-1775 (b39399a) and Q-1776 (c45ca91). The changes:
  - claim 17: every run end that does not go straight into another run gives the aircraft an
    edge destination, with its draws;
  - claim 32: a target that leaves the map is lost at that moment;
  - claim 34: the exit-flight behaviours ignore other requests;
  - claim 35: the slot aircraft's interval between a run and the next launch pass;
  - claim 37: the target-loss draw;
  - claim 40: the selection at every *move*'s first processing;
  - new claims 45 (after the run, with no dock types) and 46 (nowhere to land);
  - rows of tables T1, T2 and T3;
  - the switch `quirks.crash_drops_planted_bomb`;
  - AC19's Q-1755 parts and scenario AC29;
  - Changes for S-0215, S-0309, S-0312 and S-0519.
- **S-0318 revision 13** (1cb0178; Q-1825 answered by 07135e3). Claim 5 and State and ordering
  spread a burst's fragment hits over several ticks by S-0203 claim 10's removal skip. SH1
  part a gains readings and a second run; SH4 and SH6 follow.
- **S-0503 revision 16** (a33bca9, against revision 7). Claim 10 covers a repair depot's
  switch-offs and switch-ons; a repeated switch-off replaces an active spin count; the stop
  request never touches the order-level destination. Claim 11 covers a depot's or naval yard's
  switch-off over water. Backing and confidence now come per sub-claim. It does not answer
  Q-1773 (a hover's stored path after a deploy order), so the `orders.rs` interim stays.

The switch `crash_drops_planted_bomb` (default on, the original) came first, alone (c531063),
and is now consulted. New hashed state, `HoverState::stopped`, and changed aircraft behaviour:
save format 73 → 74, AI game format 34 → 35 (on top of intake 8). The sim-gate goldens were
regenerated; five of the nine move (see *Measurements*).

**Code.**

- **`air::run_end`** (claim 17). After the target is forgotten and before *retreat* or the
  fall-back, the run end sets a destination: a cell on the house's computed edge, chosen by
  `arrival_edge_cell` with S-0522 claim 4's draws.
- **`air::fall_back_rows`** (claim 18, T2).
  - The row's behaviour is chosen first and queued last, so the after-the-row entries can
    withhold it.
  - The in-flight *guard* row keeps the destination. It gives *area guard* for a computer
    house's armed aircraft, as the ground row does.
  - The after-the-row pad search follows every row from the fourth on, the visiting rows
    included.
  - When that search finds no pad and the type is pad-bound, nothing is queued. In flight the
    aircraft crashes; on the ground it keeps its current behaviour.
  - The ground row forgets target and destination (unchanged; now cited).
- **`air::crash_fall_back`** (claim 46, new). It replaces the old living crash
  `start_crash(w, id, true)`, which has no caller in this path any more.
  - Health is set to 0. `death::mark_killed`, then `damage::credit` with an empty hit
    (credited to nobody, one loss of its own house).
  - `destruction_raises`, then `start_crash_hit(.., false, None)`: passengers lost, three
    tumble draws, no debris, no explosion-list pick, no bomb detonation.
  - It falls as a dead aircraft; S-0312 claim 16's demolition damage does not reach it.
- **`air::impact`** (claim 46, Edge cases). A dead aircraft still carrying a bomb: with the
  switch on the bomb is dropped silently; off, `bomb::detonate` runs before the death weapon.
- **`tick::object_turn`** (claim 46, S-0519 claim 4). A crashing aircraft's bomb timer runs
  (`bomb::turn`) before its flight in its turn. The detonate order works as for any carrier.
- **`air::targeters_lose`** (claim 32, new). Called from `objects::take_inside` and
  `objects::remove`. Every aircraft targeting the object takes claim 37's draw, holds no target
  and is reverted if overridden (S-0215 claim 10). On a removal a target the revert brings back
  is cleared, since the object no longer exists.
- **`air::target_loss_draw`** (claim 37, new). It is also called from `death::mark_killed`
  before the clear. With more than 10 ticks left on the targeting timer, one *U*(4, 8) draw
  restarts it. Aircraft only: the every-actor loss is S-0309's.
- **`attack_stage`** (T1).
  - *approaching*: every processing first turns the run lock off and spends a pending round.
    No target or no ammunition gives *done* after 1 tick (it was the standard delay).
  - *firing* (no target or no ammunition) and *refiring* (no target): *done* after 1 tick
    (they returned no delay of their own).
- **`air::move_`** (claim 40). The selection runs at the first processing of every ordinary
  *move* (`beh.expired`), also for a destination already flown to. `tick_flight` no longer
  runs it on a new destination.
- **`air::guard`** (claim 29). At cruise height with a destination, *move* is queued only when
  the type is not pad-bound.
- **`air::landing_trigger`** (claims 6 and 45). A pad-bound type with no dock types and
  ammunition left keeps its cell and stays above it instead of falling back.
- **`air::area_guard`** (claim 45, new; *area guard* was processed as *guard*).
  - At or above the flight height: the fall-back, delay 1 (Q-1837 interim for above).
  - Below the flight height: the spent-ammunition fall-back first. Then a held target queues
    *attack* (delay 1); otherwise S-0215 claim 26 runs (`process::process_area_guard`, now with
    *k* = 2 for aircraft).
- **`air::enter`** (T3, claim 46). A pad that is gone clears the destination before the
  fall-back. So does a refusal when nothing is queued (Open question 19 otherwise: kept).
- **`air::queue_ignored`** (claim 34, new). Used by `air::queue`, the landing trigger's
  waypoint *wait* and `orders::apply_behaviour`. An aircraft that is not a strike aircraft, in
  one of the five exit behaviours, drops any other request: no queue, no start. The order's
  other effects (target, destination, contact) still apply.
- **`hover::stop_request`** (S-0503 claim 10). It clears the path and the locomotor's *D*
  (`retained`, `held`, and the new `stopped` mark) and leaves the order-level destination in
  place. `moving` and `destination_point` read *D* as absent while the destination is still
  the marked one. The next destination taken, or stopping, completion or failure, ends the
  mark. `destination_point` is now `pub` for the test.
- **S-0318 and the rest of S-0503**: no code change. The engine already spread fragment hits by
  the removal skip, which Q-1825 asked about. A depot's switch-offs and switch-ons (`depot.rs`)
  and the replacement of the spin count (`hover::switched` sets the count anew) were built
  already.

  loss is not yet revised). `death::mark_killed` clears every actor's target as before.

  mark.

  Claim 4's push-back then keeps a visiting aircraft inside the usable area, so the tests that
  need a removal use `banded_game`.

  as T4's note says for slot aircraft.

## Local lane

No stock data is needed for these tests. By eye, in the native build:

- Build an armed aircraft type without dock types (or one whose pads are all sold) and attack
  with it. After its run it now turns toward its house's map edge. A human player's lands a few
  cells inside the edge and stays; it no longer circles over the target.
- An aircraft that must return to a pad and has none crashes at once when its run ends. It
  falls dead, and the score screen counts one loss and no kill.
- An aircraft whose target boards a transport flies home with its ammunition.
