# S-0701 r35 and S-0703 r58: claim ledger, M8 step A3 intake 25

This intake takes in two audio spec revisions that carry the lead's ruling of 2026-10-24
(docs/m8-plan.md, *Lead rulings*): while the options menu is open in a loaded skirmish the music
plays on, as in the original, with music time on the host's monotonic clock and lost focus still
a full stand-still. It reverses intake 12's options-menu hold. The ruling's other S-0703 item, the
end screen at game over, was built at intake 12 and is now accepted.

- **S-0701 revision 35** (spec commit 3cb4553, from revision 27 built at intake 12, 542db92).
  Claim 30 gains *Music time* (*M*, *K*, music-only passes in the options menu, lost focus
  stopping *M*) and its list of what depends on real time; the options-menu stand-still is one for
  sound only; H2 gains the menu's clock readings as logical input, a focus control and an
  options-menu control. The scope paragraph, the audio-scheduling departure and the closing
  paragraph name the one accepted real-time presentation exception. Changes for other specs gains
  the glossary amendment (*departure*, *audio clock*). The rest is wording ("this revision's" to
  "this spec's", hand-off confirmations dropped).
- **S-0703 revision 58** (6d8b87e, from revision 51, 1fdad4b). Claim 10 runs a music update at
  each music-only pass and counts a track's duration on music time; claim 13's own-engine
  paragraph plays the music on in the menu and on our settings screen opened from a game; the
  State and ordering row and the MU11 part f engine fixture follow; the options-menu departure is
  dropped and the end screen's is accepted; the two lead items of Open questions are settled.
  Every rating of a design rule that only our fixture checks drops from high to medium. Changes
  for other specs rework the network-game form of the surrender runs (S-0414, S-0201, S-0313,
  S-0308, S-0508, S-0311, S-0601, S-0109, S-0413, S-0203, S-0011): those are other specs'
  revisions, taken in by their own intakes (intake 24 takes S-0201 r31 and S-0414 r30).

No quirk switch is named or changed (`quirks.music_fallback_entry_zero` unchanged;
`every_rendering_spec_quirk_has_a_switch` green). No simulation code changed:
`git diff main -- crates/deterministic crates/sim-gate` is empty; formats and goldens are
unchanged. No request is retired: no Q-id carried the hold (it was revision 51's own departure),
and no interim cites these claims. No request filed (block Q-1925 to Q-1929 unused).

**Code.**
- `glasnost_audio::stand` (new): `Stand` (`Play`, `Menu`, `Unfocused`) and `HeldClock`, a
  host-time clock that leaves its stopped stretches out (from its last counted reading to the
  first reading after it runs again).
- `glasnost_audio::events::Audio`:
  - `frame_in` and `service_in` take the frame's stand; `frame` and `service` are `Stand::Play`.
  - Two clocks: the sound clock (host time less the options menu and lost focus, fed to
    `Mixer::service` and the announcer) and music time *M* (host time less lost focus).
  - `Stand::Menu`: no audio pass, no announcement, sound held, music not held, and a music-only
    pass when *M* − *K* > 33 ms that runs the music update and nothing else.
  - `Stand::Unfocused`: both clocks stopped, sound and music held, no pass.
  - *K* is set at each audio pass and each music-only pass; `stand`, `music_held`, `sounds_held`.
  - `hold_music` is gone.
- `glasnost_audio::mixer::Mixer`: `hold_sounds` (render skips the 16 channels and the
  announcement voice, so no sample advances or ends and no per-instance draw is taken),
  `sounds_held`, `music_held`.
- `game::client`: the game frame's stand (a network game is always `Play`; a skirmish without
  focus `Unfocused`; its options menu `Menu`); our settings screen opened from a game runs
  `frame_in` with `Menu` (or `Unfocused` without focus in a skirmish), so it now also pumps the
  output.
- Tests: `crates/audio/src/music_tests.rs` (`mu11f_the_options_menu_plays_the_music_on`,
  `h2_lost_focus_in_the_options_menu_adds_nothing_to_music_time`, `lost_focus_holds_the_music`,
  `mu11e_leaving_through_the_options_menu` updated), `crates/audio/src/mixer_tests.rs`
  (`held_sounds_resume_from_the_same_point`), `crates/audio/src/stand.rs`
  (`a_stopped_stretch_adds_nothing`), `crates/app/src/game/client_music_tests.rs`
  (`the_options_menu_and_the_settings_screen_play_the_music_on`, `lost_focus_holds_the_music`).
  Every test injects the host clock; none sleeps.

**Design rules of our own** (Decisions of 2026-10-25), each with a `// design:` comment:
- A network game stands still neither for its options menu nor for lost focus (S-0201 claims 15
  and 16), so its audio stays play; S-0701 claim 30 covers a skirmish. Its settings screen opened
  from the game plays the music on as the options menu does, also without focus.
- A menu opened before the game's first audio pass (no *K* yet) makes its music-only pass at its
  first reading: our host-time clock has no *K* = 0 at loading.
