# S-0405 r36, S-0316 r35 and three more: claim ledger, M8 step A3 intake 11

This intake takes in five spec revisions. None of them answers a request: the `requests:` fields
are unchanged, so no interim is retired.

- **S-0405 revision 36** (spec commit db2a3a3).
  - Claim 9: a building out of operation keeps its working animations, except as the rendering
    spec's power rules change them. A sold or packed-up building's working animations stay drawn
    until the first tick of its build-down. Its light changes only at the events the claim lists.
    A searchlight beam is drawn on no frame while its building is out of operation.
  - Claim 10: an accepted switch-off freezes the *freeze-unpowered* slots (renderer
    `quirks.enable_unfreezes_unpowered`). An ownership change leaves the switch as it is.
  - Claim 20: a disabled hovering vehicle still above the ground can accept a scatter request.
    Its arrival is an arrival in claim 13's sense, so it gets the disabling again, with no sound.
  - A sell order to a type with no build-up sprite starts no sale.
  - New scenario W15.
  - Changes for other specs: S-0207 claim 16 case (g) for a hovering vehicle, and S-0108 claim 10
    with scenario B14 reversed.
- **S-0316 revision 35** (1809cef). Claim 2: the ranking of the line-effect flags is for shots.
  A fragment's bolt is made whatever the radiation-beam and magnetic-beam flags. A fragment makes
  no radiation beam and no magnetic wave while its projectile has its attacker and the attacker
  has no live wave; the other cases are open. Claims 8 and 10: no bolt once the attacker has gone,
  and the shrapnel weapon's own alternate-colour flag colours it.
- **S-0303 revision 25** (ea94b71). Row 1a and the sentence on S-0306 claim 21's draws are
  cross-references to S-0318 and S-0306. Claim 19: a barrel debris piece takes its creation draws
  after the draw that chose it and before the particle system's percent draw. Claim 22: F6's
  voxel-model debris consists of pieces. Claim 23: a debris piece's end is a second producer of
  area damage only, with the warhead's light flash.
- **S-0413 revision 6** (6b844f6). Scenario C3 part a's game now ends with the human's defeat
  at a fixed tick. Wording only.
- **S-0616 revision 20** (c4c1e6e). Claim 1: a voxel-model animation's system is made at the
  piece's creation point, and keeps the displacement S-0317 claim 13 gives.

The only quirk switch named is the renderer's `enable_unfreezes_unpowered`, which main already
lists (1bc5325).

**Code.**
- `scatter::vehicle_request`, case (g):
  - A hovering vehicle is refused while `!hover::powered`, that is, switched off and down at
    height 0.
  - Any other vehicle is refused while disabled (S-0405 claims 13 and 20: the disabling switches
    its locomotor off).
  - Before, only a switched-off hover was refused, so a disabled drive vehicle could accept a
    request. S-0405 claim 20 already said it could not.
- `econ::power::arrival` no longer skips a vehicle that is already disabled. `disable` gives the
  guard reset, the deselection and the switch-off, and plays no sound for a vehicle that was
  already disabled.
- `mapview::live::slot_draws` draws no slot of a building whose sale is in `BuildDown`.
- The rest is tests and citations.

New hashed state: none. Formats: save 74, AI game 35, unchanged. Goldens: see the step's report.
