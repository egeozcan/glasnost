## Built

- **The warp-pending answer** (S-0513 claim 39, Changes for S-0301). `combat::check::check`
  answers *illegal* about a target that has a superweapon warp pending, from the launch until its
  departure update (`superweapons::warp_pending_or_leaving`: the pending stage and the leaving
  stage), for every weapon. The temporal exception of r43 is gone (Q-2213). The test keeps its
  place in rank 4, just before the held-spawner *impossible*: after every other rank-4 reason
  (the materialising firer's *rearming*, the held-temporal-target *rearming* of S-0514 claim 19,
  the hidden-target *impossible*, and the rest Changes for S-0301 lists), and before the spawner
  answers, the S-0306 claim 23 rows, the live-effect *impossible*, the magnetic-beam *rearming*
  and ranks 5 to 16. `superweapons::warp_leaving` is no longer called by the check (it stays
  public).
- **The holders' drops** come from code that was already there: intake 68's
  `combat::held::periodic_check` drops a refused target at the first check tick (tick number a
  multiple of 16) after the launch for a player house's holder in *attack* or *guard*, with no
  draw, no revert and no behaviour change; the next *attack* processing finds no target and ends
  the attack; an opportunity target goes at the next search (S-0309 claims 4 and 19); an infantry
  holder's firing step keeps the target and makes no shot. Since the launch order is applied
  after the turn pass of *t*_L (S-0203 claim 7), the first check tick falls in
  (*t*_L, *t*_L + 16], as claim 39 gives.
- **Comments.** `combat::held` (`left`, `firing_target`, `turn_start_drop`) and
  `tick::object_turn` now cite r62 and the interim of Q-2211 question 3.
- **Our arena's set-up** (S-0216 claim 8). In `crates/tools/src/oracle.rs`:
  - `setup` places side A's units by index, then side B's. `place_unit` creates a unit, tests
    its stated cell, moves it on if refused, places it and queues *hunt*, all before the next
    unit is created.
  - `refusal` is the take test: inside the usable area by the height-aware test
    (`cells.usable`), entry outcome *clear* (claim 5), and for a vehicle no unit on the cell; for
    infantry, no first-row refusal (`no_position_by_flags`) and one of the right, left and
    bottom positions free. It takes no draw. Any other kind is refused (aircraft groups, below).
  - `placed_cell` tries the stated cell, then `neighbours` in claim 8's order (`NEIGHBOURS`); if
    none takes the unit, `place_unit` returns an error and `setup` stops with it.
  - The placement is `objects::place_in_play` (claims 1 to 4: the facing, infantry's R(0, 3)
    standing position, the draws in their order) and then `beh.queue(Some(Behaviour::Hunt))`.
  - `count` is claim 8's formula: an exact half to the even count, then the minimum and the
    infantry cap.
  - Removed: the rings (`ring`, `largest_ring`), the M-0006 override (`Ground::placed` and its
    reading through `load_placements` in `stock_ground` and the CLI), and A1's interim (cell
    centre, no standing-position draw, *hunt* forced). `load_placements` and `placed_for` stay for
    the acceptance check.

## Design choices

- The take test's infantry part asks `occupancy::no_position_by_flags` and the occupancy's
  `full()` for the three side positions; a cell holding infantry still takes a later infantry
  unit while one of them is free (first come, first served).
- A refused unit's error names the unit and the stated cell. A unit of a type that is neither
  infantry nor vehicle is refused on every cell, so its group stops the set-up with an error;
  no record has one.
