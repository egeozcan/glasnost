# S-0612 claim ledger, M8 step B3 (the dominator's aim)

The own AI's use of the psychic dominator (S-0612, revision 2 of the spec, as at M7b; the simulation
side is unchanged and its ledger is [../m7-claims/S-0612.md](../m7-claims/S-0612.md)). This ledger covers
what the aim reads off the claims: which units a launch takes, where it fires, and when it holds.
Tests: `crates/deterministic/ai/tests/dominator.rs` (16), `the_hit_estimate_is_the_detonation_loss` in
`crates/deterministic/sim/tests/claims_s0303.rs`, the lane tally test
`counts_dominator_launches_takeovers_and_damage` (`crates/tools/tests/tally.rs`).
Code: `glasnost_ai::dominator` (`dominate_cell`, `best_take`, `best_damage`, `worth`, `blast_hit`,
`takeable`, `standing`, `wrecks`, `holding`, `probe`), `glasnost_sim::combat::damage::hit_estimate` and
`glasnost_sim::combat::area::object_distances` (estimates that change nothing), the lane's sweep
counter and launch and sweep lines (`crates/tools/src/skirmish_lane.rs`).
