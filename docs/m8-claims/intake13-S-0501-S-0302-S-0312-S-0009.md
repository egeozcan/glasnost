# S-0501 r41, S-0302 r39, S-0312 r44 and S-0009 r44: claim ledger, M8 step A3 intake 13

This intake takes in four spec revisions. Three of them answer requests, so three interims are
retired: Q-1837 and Q-1838 (S-0501) and Q-1805 (S-0009).

- **S-0501 revision 41** (spec commit 338427e; Q-1837 answered in 5fc1a6a, Q-1838 in 0fab590).
  - Claim 45: each *area guard* processing compares the aircraft's height, taken at the start of
    the processing, with its cruise height. Only equality gives the fall-back to rest with a
    delay of 1 tick. Above the cruise height the processing takes the branch stated for below it.
  - Terms and claim 1: the placement fall-back comes before the type test that marks an aircraft
    visiting. A type marked only by that test is placed on the ground inside the usable area. It
    rests in *guard* (*area guard* for an armed aircraft of a computer house outside a team) and
    takes orders and launch passes. A later fall-back on the ground takes T2's visiting rows.
  - New scenario parts AC29 g (4) and j, and a placement record in part h.
- **S-0302 revision 39** (2c13d4c). Claims 1 and 6 to 8: a hit without a warhead, from a debris
  piece of a type with no warhead or a scripted source. No row of claim 8 that reads the warhead
  holds for it: neither bunker row, none of the three immunity rows, not the allies row and not
  the berserk row. Its *m*₀ is 0, and claim 7 raises that to 1 on an undemolishable building.
  It costs readiness. New scenario D34 parts a to e.
- **S-0312 revision 44** (f9236b1). It takes in the S-0312 entries of S-0317's and S-0501's
  Changes for other specs.
  - Claim 4: voxel debris consists of S-0317 pieces. Their hits carry no attacking house.
  - Claim 7: a carrier destroyed without a hit keeps its planted bomb through the fall.
  - Claim 16: the fall-back crash takes the three tumbling draws and none of the killing-hit
    effects. New scenario D11 variant g.
- **S-0009 revision 44** (54806eb; Q-1805 answered in 6f99ced).
  - Claim 19: the house order of several humans is by colour slot. A human on "random" is placed
    by the slot S-0412 claim 7 gave it, so the colour draws come before the house order.
  - Our engine's design rules: ties go to the earlier lobby slot; observers go between the humans
    and the computers, in lobby-slot order; a network game's random colours are drawn for every
    player in increasing lobby-slot order.
  - New Import mapping row `[Basic] Theme`.
  - Confidence and backing changes for network-only statements.

No revision names a quirk without a switch. `every_spec_quirk_has_a_switch` passes.

**Code.**
- `air::area_guard`: `h == t.cruise` replaces the interim `!on_ground && h >= t.cruise`.
- `air::placed` no longer sets the visiting mark. The new `air::placed_type_test` sets it, and
  `objects::place` calls it after `process::fall_back(.., true)` and `refresh::placement_end`.
  The placement height and the placement fall-back see only a mark set before placement. Paradrop
  planes, spy planes and strike aircraft set it before placement, so they are unchanged.
- `air::step`: the claim 10 removal test also runs after claim 4's descent nudge, so an aircraft
  whose nudge carries it out of the usable area is removed in that tick ("at once"). This came
  to light when the Q-1838 workaround in
  `lost_target_visiting_hunts_toward_the_edge_and_is_removed_at_the_border` was retired. A type
  that is not landable keeps the hold mark (claim 6), so it descends toward its edge cell, and its
  nudge crossed the border one tick before the forward-step test saw it.
- `combat::damage::apply`: the bunker rows read the warhead's penetration flag as an `Option`.
  A hit without a warhead no longer meets the bunkered-unit row, which `!penetrates` used to
  match.
- `econ::setup::draw_colour`: S-0412 claim 7's draw, taken out of `resolve_lobby`, which now
  calls it.
- `ui::Setup::resolve_colours(seed)`: gives every occupied row on "random" a free colour slot,
  persons and computers alike, in increasing row order. The stream is `Rng::new(seed + 1)`, so it
  does not repeat the country draws of `resolve_lobby`'s stream. `Setup::house_order` is unchanged,
  apart from its doc and citations.
- `net::lobby::Room::start`: resolves the random colours on a copy of the room's setup before the
  house order. The resolved setup travels in `Begin.setup`, so every client sees fixed colours.
  The room's own setup keeps its "random" choices. The skirmish path is unchanged: one person,
  whose place is first whatever its colour.
- Citations only: `combat::death::debris` and `pieces` (S-0312 claim 4), `mapview::load`
  (`[Basic] Theme`), and the bomb tests of claim 46 (S-0312 claim 7).

## Seams

- Observers: the lobby has no observer rows, so claim 19's place for observers and claim 22's
  shown position are not built.
- AC29 part h's launch record (height above the water, *guard*, the next pass's rows) is not
  checked step by step; the end-to-end fight is.
- `Setup::resolve_colours` runs only in the network room. A skirmish has one person, whose place
  is first whatever its colour, so its colour draws stay in `resolve_lobby` as before.
