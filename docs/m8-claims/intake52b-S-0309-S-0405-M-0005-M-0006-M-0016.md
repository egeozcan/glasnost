# S-0309 r51, S-0405 r47, M-0005, M-0006, M-0016: claim ledger, M8 step A3 intake 52b

This intake takes in two revisions and three measurement records:

- **S-0309 r51** (target acquisition) answers **Q-2006**, the request that
  [intake38-M-0011.md](intake38-M-0011.md) filed for a crusher that fires at the target it has
  just crushed. Claim 27 is new: when an object is destroyed, goes off the map or is removed, every
  infantry unit, vehicle, building and aircraft that holds it as its target drops it inside that
  action. A holder whose targeting timer has more than 10 ticks left restarts it with one ranged
  draw over 4 to 8 ticks. Claim 28 gives the cloak drops (S-0516 claim 14) and the drop on going
  underground the same draw. It also covers **Q-2114** ([a6-arena-triage.md](a6-arena-triage.md),
  *Found on the way*: the targets of a crushed unit).
- **S-0405 r47** (control-centre units) answers **Q-1710** with M-0005. Claim 13's *Hovering
  vehicles* paragraph says the arrivals of a hovering vehicle are the cell-arrival updates of its
  heading-point choices, read on the cell it stands in. A placed hovering vehicle is disabled at its
  first such choice, still on its placement cell. Claim 20 says a disabled vehicle is targeted,
  damaged and destroyed like any other.
- **M-0005** replays the twelve LTNK against ROBO games of M-0001 on stock data (the stall).
- **M-0006** replays archived games and gives the map's neutral bunkers, W, and the cells that the
  units stated on the five cliff cells were placed on (Q-1711, Q-1713).
- **M-0016** replays every archived game with SREF or V3 on stock data. Its 48 changed rows
  replace the archived ones, so with M-0017 every pairing is scored.

No quirk comes with either revision. Intake 52a (S-0210, S-0215, S-0216 and the quirk
`auto_crush_computer_only`) ran at the same time. This intake does not touch those specs: claim 27
cites S-0215 claim 10's revert and S-0210 claim 4's crush as they stand on main.

## Built

- **`combat::held`** (new) holds claim 27's drop for every kind of holder:
  - `holders_drop(w, gone, how)`. For each holder in key order, the timer draw comes first
    (`timer_draw`). Then the target is cleared, or a revert (S-0215 claim 10) restores the target
    put aside. An aircraft keeps S-0501 claim 32's order: clear, then revert. A removal clears a
    target the revert gave back again, with no second draw. The burst counter is reset, and a
    restored destination is assigned.
  - `timer_draw(w, id)`. With more than 10 ticks left, one `ranged(4, 8)` from the scenario
    stream sets the timer to that many ticks from now. It replaces the aircraft-only
    `air::target_loss_draw` (S-0501 claim 37), which S-0309 claim 27 now gives every holder.
  - `firing_target(w, o)` and `turn_start_drop(w, id)` hold the DECIDED rule. A held object that
    is off the map, or destroyed but still in play, is no target in the firing step. It is dropped
    at the start of the holder's next turn, with no draw and no revert.
- **The callers.**
  - `combat::death::mark_killed` drops at the killing hit (`Leaving::Destroyed`). A target that
    the revert restores is no longer cleared at the kill.
  - `objects::take_inside` drops at an off-map move (`Leaving::OffMap`).
  - `objects::remove` drops at a removal, a crush included (`Leaving::Removed`).
  - `air::targeters_lose` is gone: its aircraft-only drop is now `holders_drop`.
  - The firing steps of `combat::behave` read `firing_target`: `vehicle_step`, `infantry_step`,
    a building's attack and `delayed_shot_turn`.
  - `tick::object_turn` calls `turn_start_drop` after the deactivated-vehicle sparks, before the
    warp turn.
  - `cloak::machine::drop_attackers` takes the timer draw for each holder that drops, at both
    cloak moments. A holder kept by an exception takes none.
- **S-0405 r47** needed no code. `hover.rs` already makes the cell-arrival update at each
  heading-point choice (`cloak::machine::cell_movement_completed` → `econ::power::arrival`) on the
  cell it stands in. The citation now names revision 47. The new tests run it.
- **The oracle tooling** (`crates/tools`):
  - `oracle::load_stock_replays` reads M-0016's stock rows, and `load_records` applies them.
    `report()` marks the pairings scored through them `[stock rows of M-0016]` and gives their
    count. `OracleRecord::scores` and `PairingComparison::by_replay` carry this.
  - `oracle::load_placements` reads M-0006's placement table, W and neutral cells.
    `Ground::placed` puts a unit stated on a table cell on its measured cell, even where a unit
    already stands. `Moved::why` names M-0006 there.
  - `oracle_trace` shows each unit's disabled state and its cell changes (`--trace`).
- **Formats.** No state was added: every new rule is read from the target's own state (`on_map`,
  `damage::dead`). The save format stays 88 and the AI game format stays 50.
