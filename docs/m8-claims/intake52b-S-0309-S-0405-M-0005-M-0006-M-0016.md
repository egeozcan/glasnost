# S-0309 r51 and r53, S-0210 r31, S-0405 r47, M-0005, M-0006, M-0016: claim ledger, M8 step A3 intake 52b

This intake takes in four revisions and three measurement records. S-0309 r53 and S-0210 r31
came during the step and are built on top of r51 (section *r53 and r31* below):

- **S-0309 r51** (target acquisition) answers **Q-2006**, the request that
  [intake38-M-0011.md](intake38-M-0011.md) filed for a crusher that fires at the target it has
  just crushed. Claim 27 is new: when an object is destroyed, goes off the map or is removed, every
  infantry unit, vehicle, building and aircraft that holds it as its target drops it inside that
  action. A holder whose targeting timer has more than 10 ticks left restarts it with one ranged
  draw over 4 to 8 ticks. Claim 28 gives the cloak drops (S-0516 claim 14) and the drop on going
  underground the same draw. It also covers **Q-2114** ([a6-arena-triage.md](a6-arena-triage.md),
  *Found on the way*: the targets of a crushed unit).
- **S-0405 r47** (control-centre units) answers **Q-1710** with M-0005. Claim 13's *Hovering
  vehicles* paragraph says the arrivals of a hovering vehicle are the cell-arrival updates of its
  heading-point choices, read on the cell it stands in. A placed hovering vehicle is disabled at its
  first such choice, still on its placement cell. Claim 20 says a disabled vehicle is targeted,
  damaged and destroyed like any other.
- **M-0005** replays the twelve LTNK against ROBO games of M-0001 on stock data (the stall).
- **M-0006** replays archived games and gives the map's neutral bunkers, W, and the cells that the
  units stated on the five cliff cells were placed on (Q-1711, Q-1713).
- **M-0016** replays every archived game with SREF or V3 on stock data. Its 48 changed rows
  replace the archived ones, so with M-0017 every pairing is scored.

- **S-0309 r53** answers **Q-2112** in part and **Q-2114**. Terms gain *creation order*: several
  holders of one object draw in creation order, whatever their update order. One crush removing
  several objects removes them one at a time in the cell layer's listing order, every draw of one
  object before the next one's. Claim 7: a give-up of the move (S-0212 claim 23 row 2) also drops
  the target. Claim 22: a driving vehicle's other route failures drop a target that is not within
  reach and set the limited-scan mark; only an empty search clears it.
- **S-0210 r31** answers **Q-2114** for the crush: a crushed vehicle shows a killed vehicle's
  explosion and destruction animations, one list pick each (S-0312 claim 9), before its holders'
  timer draws. A crushed infantry unit, building or aircraft shows none.

No quirk comes with any revision. Intake 52a (S-0210 r29, S-0215, S-0216 and the quirk
`auto_crush_computer_only`) ran at the same time and is on main; this branch was rebased onto it
(main 6060b8b, with intake 53), then onto main 6267666 (intake 54) and 3bf33ef (A4 round 6, S-0318 r25). S-0210 r31's change to claim 11's wording (the neutral and special

## Built

- **`combat::held`** (new) holds claim 27's drop for every kind of holder:
  - `holders_drop(w, gone, how)`. For each holder in key order (creation order, r53), the timer
    draw comes first
    (`timer_draw`). Then the target is cleared, or a revert (S-0215 claim 10) restores the target
    put aside. An aircraft keeps S-0501 claim 32's order: clear, then revert. A removal clears a
    target the revert gave back again, with no second draw. The burst counter is reset, and a
    restored destination is assigned.
  - `timer_draw(w, id)`. With more than 10 ticks left, one `ranged(4, 8)` from the scenario
    stream sets the timer to that many ticks from now. It replaces the aircraft-only
    `air::target_loss_draw` (S-0501 claim 37), which S-0309 claim 27 now gives every holder.
  - `firing_target(w, o)` and `turn_start_drop(w, id)` hold the DECIDED rule. A held object that
    is off the map, or destroyed but still in play, is no target in the firing step. It is dropped
    at the start of the holder's next turn, with no draw and no revert.
- **The callers.**
  - `combat::death::mark_killed` drops at the killing hit (`Leaving::Destroyed`). A target that
    the revert restores is no longer cleared at the kill.
  - `objects::take_inside` drops at an off-map move (`Leaving::OffMap`).
  - `objects::remove` drops at a removal, a crush included (`Leaving::Removed`).
  - `air::targeters_lose` is gone: its aircraft-only drop is now `holders_drop`.
  - The firing steps of `combat::behave` read `firing_target`: `vehicle_step`, `infantry_step`,
    a building's attack and `delayed_shot_turn`.
  - `tick::object_turn` calls `turn_start_drop` after the deactivated-vehicle sparks, before the
    warp turn.
  - `cloak::machine::drop_attackers` takes the timer draw for each holder that drops, at both
    cloak moments. A holder kept by an exception takes none.
- **S-0405 r47** needed no code. `hover.rs` already makes the cell-arrival update at each
  heading-point choice (`cloak::machine::cell_movement_completed` → `econ::power::arrival`) on the
  cell it stands in. The citation now names revision 47. The new tests run it.
- **The oracle tooling** (`crates/tools`):
  - `oracle::load_stock_replays` reads M-0016's stock rows, and `load_records` applies them.
    `report()` marks the pairings scored through them `[stock rows of M-0016]` and gives their
    count. `OracleRecord::scores` and `PairingComparison::by_replay` carry this.
  - `oracle::load_placements` reads M-0006's placement table, W and neutral cells.
    `Ground::placed` puts a unit stated on a table cell on its measured cell, even where a unit
    already stands. `Moved::why` names M-0006 there.
  - `oracle_trace` shows each unit's disabled state and its cell changes (`--trace`).
- **r53 and r31** (commit `sim: a crushed vehicle's animation picks, …`):
  - `combat::death::vehicle_animations` is the killed vehicle's two picks, taken out of
    `death::kill` unchanged. `crush::at_cell_centre` calls it for a crushed vehicle before
    `objects::remove`, so the picks come before the holders' draws, and each victim's draws come
    before the next victim's (the loop already removes them one at a time in listing order).
  - `combat::held::holders_drop`'s key order is creation order: `World::new_id` gives keys out in
    creation order and never reuses them, and re-entering play does not change an object's key.
  - `path::failed`: at a give-up of S-0212 claim 23 row 2 the target is cleared with the burst
    counter (claim 7). At any other failed search (gate or search failure) of a vehicle whose
    current locomotor is the driving one, a held target that is not within reach is dropped and
    `combat.limited_scan` is set (claim 22). The mark's effect and its clearing by an empty search
    were already built (`acquire::search`); until now nothing set it.
  - `combat::acquire::within_reach` is claim 23's *within reach*, taken out of the attack-move
    search, which now calls it.
- **Formats.** No state was added: every new rule is read from the target's own state (`on_map`,
  `damage::dead`), and the limited-scan mark was already saved and hashed. The save format stays
  89 and the AI game format stays 51 (intake 54 set them; this intake changes neither).

## Design choices

| question | our choice |
|---|---|
| The order of several holders' timer draws | Creation order (S-0309 r53 Terms and claim 27), which is our key order: no code change |
| "Standing still" after a route failure (S-0309 claim 22) | Every failed search leaves the vehicle stopped (S-0212 claim 23: the unit stops), a re-path for a blocked step included, so `path::failed` does not read the vehicle's speed |
| A gate failure (S-0212 claim 23 gate row) under claim 22 | Counted with the failures that do not give the move up, so it sets the mark: claim 22 names only row 2 and S-0207 claim 15 as give-ups |
| The vehicles of claim 22 | The current locomotor (`lift::current_locomotor`) is the driving one, so a seized unit's driving stand-in counts. The hovering and ship locomotors set no mark (conditions not examined in the spec) |
| A crushed vehicle's last-entry rule | The killed vehicle's: its own ammunition and the explodes flag or ability, read before the removal |
| The draws of claim 27 at a killing hit against the death effects' own draws (S-0309 Open questions) | With the hit, before every death-effect draw: `mark_killed` comes first in `combat::death::kill`, as the spec leans |
| A warp departure (S-0513 claim 39) is a way off the map, but its drop is S-0207 claim 20 e's, which takes no draw | Kept: `teleport::targeters_drop`, no draw. **Q-2156**, answered by S-0513 r43 (intake 60): the departure drops nobody and takes no draw, so `warp::depart` no longer calls `targeters_drop` ([intake60-S-0513.md](intake60-S-0513.md)) |
| An aircraft holder after the turn-start drop of the DECIDED rule | The target is cleared, nothing more. The behaviour that follows belongs to the aircraft specs (S-0309 claim 27) |
| A measured placement onto a cell where a unit already stands (M-0006) | Taken as measured. The other moved units still use `oracle::placed_cell` (Q-2096) |
