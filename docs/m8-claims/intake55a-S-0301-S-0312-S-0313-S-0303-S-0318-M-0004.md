## Built

- **The building refill** (S-0301 claim 10). `combat::fire::building_refill` sets a building at 0
  rounds to its type's maximum *A*, unless the type has the `Hospital` or `Armory` flag (read by
  `convert::sim` into the building's raw fields; absent no). *A* = −1 gives −1 (unlimited), *A* = 0
  gives 0. `tick::object_turn` calls it in the building's own turn after its building branch, so
  after behaviour processing with both attempts of a paired firing (claim 9) and after an
  animation-delayed shot. No countdown and no reload field takes part. An unchecked secondary
  attempt at 0 rounds was already made with no round and claim 2's normal delay.
- **The matched emitter** (S-0301 claim 5 and Terms). `emitters::own_effect_alive` reads
  `emitters::any_held`, which is true while the firer's emitter place holds the emitter. The place
  is emptied by a let-go (`let_go_fire`) and by the system's leaving play (`release_places`), so a
  matched emitter's life ends at the first of the two, as r58 states. The citations now name r58;
  an emitter that is not matched follows the same rule as an interim (**Q-2181**; answered by
  S-0301 r63, [intake 63](intake63-S-0213-S-0301.md): the place, not the system's kind, sets
  the let-go and the creation).
- **The crew** (S-0312 claim 13). `combat::death` now creates the crew (its creation draw), then
  `death::crew_position` chooses where it goes:
  - the dying vehicle's vehicle-present claims (`drive::held_claims`, taken out of
    `drive::release_claims`) and its listing in the cell are set aside, so the vehicle never refuses
    its own crew;
  - at the terrain surface height, `walk::choose_position` on the ground record with the class of
    the vehicle's exact position (S-0208 claim 2), one R(0, 3) only for a centre class the first row
    does not refuse; outside the usable area the class's own position with no draw;
  - at another height (a bridge deck), the vehicle's exact position with no draw;
  - then one entry test (`occupancy::infantry_outcome`): only *clear* accepts the crew.
  A crew with no position, or refused, is taken out of the world with no health draw. The vehicle's
  claims and listing are given back afterwards. `survivors::put_infantry` puts the crew at the
  chosen position.
- **The survivors** (S-0312 claim 18). `survivors::place_survivor` chooses with the centre class
  on the ground record (R(0, 3) unless the first row refuses), with no entry test; the destroyed
  building's own presence plays no part. The first-free-position interim of Q-2132 is gone.
- **Clear for movement** (S-0312 claim 19 (g)). `survivors::clear_for_movement` fails a foundation
  cell when the record it reads has its object-present flag on (the deck record under a high
  bridge), when the cell holds an overlay with the wall flag, or when the tracked class's
  multiplier of its land type is exactly 0. The cell's own buildings, vehicles and infantry play no
  part. It replaces Q-2133's interim (S-0211 category *open*).
- **The passenger release** (S-0312 claim 24) already tested only the no-crew state; the comment in
  `structures::absorb` now cites r51. `combat::state`'s no-crew comment cites r51's *No crew*.
- **The muzzle fields** (S-0313 claim 1b). `convert::sim_combat::muzzle_positions` reads, for
  every type read of a type, the family its turret count picks (that read's `TurretCount`):
  the numbered fields up to the weapon count above 0, the primary and secondary pair at 0 or
  less. An absent non-elite field keeps the slot's held value, an absent elite field takes the
  slot's non-elite value after that reading, and the family not read leaves its slots as they
  are. `art_triple_at` reads one reading's triple. `combat::data` reads the numbered lists only for
  a turret count above 0 and the pair only at 0 or less. The interim of Q-2146 is gone.
- **The rocking** (S-0303 claim 20). `combat::area` rocks after the object hits and before the
  bridge tests, the centre-cell exploding overlay and the particle system. `rock` pushes a
  centre-cell object from the source object whatever *S*, and every other object (the centre cell's
  included when there is no source object) from *P*, only when *S* > 0.
- **Formats.** No state is added: the refill and the crew and survivor choices read and write
  existing fields. The save format stays main's 90 and the AI game format main's 52.

## Design choices

| question | our choice |
|---|---|
| An emitter that is not matched (a fire flag with a spark-kind system, a spark flag with a fire-kind system, railgun pairings) | Treated as matched: alive while its place holds it, so the weapon fires again after the first of a let-go and its system's leaving play, and *R*′ (**Q-2181**). *Replaced by S-0301 r63 claim 5 ([intake 63](intake63-S-0213-S-0301.md)): a fire-place let-go marks whatever system the place holds; the spark and railgun places are freed only when their system leaves play; the railgun place passes no target object at creation* |
| The ticks in which the refill runs | Every object turn of a building that reaches its building branch. A building destroyed and awaiting removal, or one whose turn ends earlier (a warp-only update, a removal or a kill earlier in the turn), is not refilled in that tick. S-0301 says "every tick in which a building is in play"; none of those turns makes a firing of the building first |
| The dying vehicle's claims after the crew's placement | Given back after the choice and the entry test, so a vehicle that stays in play (a sinking one) keeps them and one that leaves play releases them there. The spec states only that the vehicle has given them up for its crew |
| The crew's entry test for a vehicle above the surface | The test of the vehicle's cell at the ground level of its cell record; the crew is put on the deck layer when the cell is a high bridge and the vehicle is above the surface |
| Jumpjet marks and other infantry positions for the crew's table | The table reads the ground record's infantry positions and vehicle claim only, as S-0208 claim 3 gives for walking |
| A rocking source object that has left play by the time of the rocking | Counts as no source object (Q-2081 question 5, as before); S-0303 r31 does not separate the two |
