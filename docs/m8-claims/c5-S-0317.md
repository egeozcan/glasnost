# S-0317 revision 34 claim ledger, M8 step C5 part 2

S-0317 revision 34 (approved 2026-10-23, answering Q-1702; spec commits 7024d78 and 368952d):
debris pieces with voxel models, from creation to removal. Four new quirk switches,
`quirks.voxel_debris_deck_impact_layer`, `quirks.voxel_debris_deck_over_water`,
`quirks.voxel_debris_trailer_height` and `quirks.voxel_debris_expiry_needs_animation` (all default
on, commit 8014595). The spec's existing switches are wired, not copied: `quirks.chop_rounding`
(S-0205), `quirks.random_magnitude_overflow` (S-0202), `quirks.update_order_removal_skip`
(S-0203) and `quirks.table_root_distance` (S-0204).

**Simulation.** `glasnost_sim::pieces` (new). `PieceRules` holds the types in registration order
with the Import mapping's defaults. A `Piece` is game state in `World::pieces` (saved and hashed:
save format 72, AI game format 33). It holds its type, house, lifetime counter, position, flight
position and velocity (singles), spin rate (double, drawing only), spin axis and creation tick.
- **Creation.** `pieces::create` takes the creation draws of claims 3 and 4 and F1, F2 and F5. It
  enters the piece in the update order (`World::update.enter_new`), makes the attached system
  directly after it (`emitters::voxel_animation_system`, the S-0616 seam, now filled) and reports
  `Effect::PieceSound`.
- **Turns.** `pieces::turn` runs from the tick pipeline's object turns (`tick::object_turn`): the
  count-down, the trailer on even ticks (F7), the flight and contacts (`fly`: gravity, the deck,
  ground, building and cliff tests, the rebound, the stop test F6, a meteor's added g), the
  impact (`land_impact`: the bounce animation, then hits on the listed objects of one cell), and
  the end (`end`: claim 11's rows, claim 12's spawns and resources, then the removal through
  `update.leave` and S-0616 claim 4's marking of its system).
- **Producers.** `combat::death::deal_debris` (S-0312 claim 4, F1, with the object's house), a
  warhead's debris in `combat::projectile` (S-0303 claim 22, F6, no house) and the barrel debris
  in `combat::area` (S-0303 claim 19, no house) all call `pieces::create_named`. A name that is
  not a listed voxel-animation type makes no piece and leaves a note.
- **Shared helpers.** Particle systems follow a piece as their owner object (`particles`,
  smoke-turn ownership). `econ::resource::add_resource_cell` opens S-0401 claim 11 to claim 12.

**Import.** `glasnost-convert` `sim_pieces` (commit 9462c6c) reads every key of a
voxel-animation section into `SimData::voxel_animations`, with the Import mapping's forms.
- **Angular limits.** The two angular limits follow S-0006 claims 3 and 5: absent from a section
  they reset to −1; a value of 0 keeps the held value.
- **Warnings.** The importer warns for the departures: a zero span (claim 16), SpawnCount
  2147483647, a resource flag without a resource overlay at position 127 (claim 12), and an empty
  splash list (claim 11).
- **Art.** `art::lookup` gives each voxel animation its own model I ‖ .vxl as role `body`, with
  no motion file (S-0007 claim 30, `art::voxel_animation`). The arctic A is added only without
  the voxel flag. An unresolved model gives no model and a warning.

**Sound.** `glasnost_audio::events` plays `Effect::PieceSound` attached at the creation point. It
moves the sound's anchor to the piece's position after each tick while the piece is in play, and
lets the sound play on when the piece ends (claim 14).

**Drawing.** `app::mapview::live` places each piece in the air layer at the end of its list when the
drawing first sees it, and drops it when it leaves play (S-0102 claims 3, 4 and 7, sort key X + Y
for claim 5). `build::live_piece` draws the own model with no motion file, turned by
`live::piece_pose`. The pose, light, palette and depth were the interim of Q-1835 (retired at intake 32).

**Requests and interim rules.** All three were retired at A3 intake 32 (S-0317 r57 answers them;
[intake32-S-0317.md](intake32-S-0317.md)). As built here:
- **Q-1834 (claim 10).**
  - A sloped cell rebounds as a flat one.
  - The turn off a cliff face negates each horizontal component along which the cell coordinate
    changed.
  - Every cell whose ground-layer occupancy holds a building counts as a building contact, and no
    overlay does.
- **Q-1835 (claim 15, drawing).**
  - The pose is the S-0105 view, then a turn about the model axis of the spin axis's largest
    component. The angle is spin rate × ticks since creation, with that component's sign, in
    whole π/16 steps modulo 32. A zero axis gets no turn, which is also the spec's stated rule.
  - S-0306 F16's diffuse light.
  - The house-colour palette of the piece's house, neutral without one, at brightness 1000.
  - No depth test and no shadow.
- **Q-1836 (Open questions, Q-1702 question 4).**
  - Every piece is placed, and none is removed for being off the map.
  - Off the map the ground height is 0, the height level 0, the land is not water and there is no
    high bridge.

**Decided without a request** (the spec states the reading, or the code needs no answer):
- The hits of one impact go to a snapshot of the cell's list taken when the hits begin, and do
  not skip an object with no health left (the spec's believed reading; S-0302 claim 8 decides
  each such hit).
- An F6 sum of 2⁵³ or more needs no special rounding: a sum that large is far above the stop
  bound, so the piece is not stopped either way.
- The attached system sits directly after its piece in the update order (the spec's own design
  rule).
- Each producer makes its pieces in the order of its own list (the spec's assumption).

**Seams.**
- The shared part of S-0007 claim 30: a voxel animation whose section sets a share flag draws
  nothing, and the importer warns. With it goes `quirks.voxel_anim_share_afresh` of S-0007's
  Edge cases, which has no switch yet.
- The warhead's light flash at a piece's end (claim 11): drawing only. No warhead light flash is
  drawn anywhere in our engine yet.
