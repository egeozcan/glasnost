# Intake 53 (rendering): claim ledger

Origin commits f8ab215..a39b5fc, rendering part. Specs as read: S-0107 r31, S-0108 r102, S-0113
r1 (new). S-0108 r102 answers Q-2001; S-0113 answers Q-2081.

**Split.** Both changes are the client's. S-0108's superweapon slots were already played by
`mapview::slot_play` from the simulation's superweapon records; the reading of a record that is
not granted moves from our interim to the spec (`mapview::live::charge_reading`). S-0113 replaces
the client's rocking (`mapview::rocking`) in full: the interim rules of Q-2081 (a damped spring,
0.05 × strength ÷ weight, the drawn-frame order) are gone. S-0113 claim 19 makes the angles and
rates game state, with fire points built on them and claim 9's destruction; our simulation does
not keep them yet, so the view applies S-0113 to the simulation's reports in the simulation's own
soft floats and rounding mode, once per game tick, so the code can move into the simulation as it
is. No simulation code changed.

**Switches.** Two new rules quirks, both default on (the original), both read only by the client:
`quirks.rocking_limit_escape` (S-0113 claim 15) and `quirks.ungranted_super_reads_clock` (S-0108
claims 11 and 18). C7's judgement is in [c7-play-fixes.md](c7-play-fixes.md#intake-53-s-0108-r102-s-0113-two-rendering-switches-judged):
the second is admitted to the play-fixes layer (off), the first is rejected.

## Seams named here

- Rocking is not game state (S-0113 claim 19): no fire points on the angles, no rolling-over
  destruction (claim 9; the view drops the body past π).
- Direct rocking and the melee link (claim 12), with table R's linked rows and the two
  coefficients.
- Aircraft bodies outside a crash (their pushes are not kept); infantry and building voxel bodies;
  the weight of kinds other than vehicles (claim 10). Crash tumble rates (claim 16) are reported
  since M8 A4 round 7.
- Crush tips and overlay additions (claim 17, S-0210 claims 6, 8 and 9) are not reported.
- `Effect::Rock` does not say which point is the source's (claim 11): the view's interim reading
  can misjudge a push's magnitude. Suggested for whoever moves rocking into the simulation: report
  the point kind with each pushed body.
- The psychic dominator ring (claim 17, open in the spec).
- S-0108: a lost record's reading rests on our S-0512 loss (intake 54); superweapon slot creation
  draws are the client's, not the scenario stream's. Claim 20's waiting row *guard* tick was
  S-0314's to check: done in intake 55b (S-0314 r107, M-0007).

## Local lane

`m7b_lane` scenes `body-rocking` and `super-ungranted` (local only, a real install).
`body-rocking`: three voxel vehicles around a rocking detonation with no source; *P* behind gives
a positive pitch rate, *P* on the left a positive roll rate and on the right a negative one, the
positive side visibly farther (claim 7's asymmetry), all three level by 30 ticks after. A lateral
push also pitches the body slightly (up to about 0.02): the trig table leaves a tiny forward
component, and claim 7's level-band pull follows it, as table R gives. `super-ungranted`: with the
superweapon setting off, each disableable superweapon building type's charge slot runs *super-3*
with the switch on and *super-1* with it off (records never granted), visible in the crops.
