# S-0318, S-0314 r73, S-0108 r60 and S-0701 r25 claim ledger, M8 step A3 intake 6

Four spec revisions. They answer Q-1714 in part, Q-1610 in part, and Q-1620 and Q-1735 in full:

- **S-0318 revision 10** (new; Q-1714 in part). Shrapnel fragments: which detonations burst into
  fragments, how many there are, where they go, and what a fragment is.
- **S-0314 revision 73** (Q-1610 in part). This revision adds:
  - the *settled vehicle* of Terms, with what makes a vehicle settled, what ends it and what it
    changes;
  - claim 6's processing spacing table;
  - claim 21's settling checks within 200 leptons and the hover switch-off and switch-on;
  - claim 22's stranding rule;
  - changes for S-0503 claim 10, S-0405 claim 20 and S-0504 claim 8.
- **S-0108 revision 60** (Q-1620). This revision adds:
  - claim 25: a replacing start keeps the stage and counts its passes afresh;
  - claim 12: the fixed restart order of a condition swap;
  - claim 20: *After a send-away* and the loss timing taken from S-0314's table.
  - Elsewhere it rewords backing and confidence.
- **S-0701 revision 25** (Q-1735). Claim 26 and the Import mapping: our caption is the entry's
  `Text` key, else its name.

Format changes: save format 70 → 71 and AI game format 31 → 32. They add new hashed state:
`Projectile::chain` (S-0318 Terms), `DepotState::settled` (was `loco_off`), `DepotState::due` and
`HoverState::off`.

All eight sim-gate goldens were regenerated (e5ec9df). The reason is the new hashed state, plus
the new play:
- detonations of a type with a fragment weapon now burst into fragments;
- the depot now follows the spacing table;
- settled vehicles now switch a hover off and on;
- a replacing slot start now keeps the stage.

## Code

**S-0318.**
- `combat::shrapnel` holds:
  - the count (F1);
  - the ring scan (claim 3), which uses S-0303's T1 order, the first-listed candidate and the
    firer's view;
  - the random cells (claim 4);
  - the fragment's fields (claim 5);
  - the launch direction (claim 6);
  - the chain bound of 16 (Edge cases, our design);
  - the beams and bolts (claim 8).
- `combat::projectile::detonate` bursts after the warhead's radiation and before the area damage.
  It bursts from the burst point (S-0306 claim 20 before (c)). Each cluster detonation bursts
  afresh. An airburst projectile bursts before its nine children.
- The quirk switches are wired: `shrapnel_first_listed_only`, `shrapnel_hits_terrain`,
  `shrapnel_building_per_cell`, plus S-0303's `spread_ring_eleven`.
- The importer reads `ShrapnelWeapon` and `ShrapnelCount`.
- The client draws a fragment's beam with thickness 1 from the burst point
  (`mapview::beams`).

**S-0314 r73.**
- `depot::settled` replaces `depot::locomotor_off`.
  - What makes a vehicle settled: `depot::movement_arrival`, the near settling processing, and
    `depot::disabled`.
  - What ends it:
    - `depot::destination_given`, called from `infantry::destination_assigned` and from the
      scatter (only for an effective assignment, S-0207 claim 27);
    - the distant moving settling check;
    - the waiting refusal;
    - `depot::enabled`;
    - the end of contact, when `quirks.depot_loss_strands_hover` is off.
  - Each of these switches a hover off or on (`hover::switched`, `HoverState::off`). A settled
    hover that is down does not travel.
- `depot::process_repair` follows claim 6's spacing table:
  - working is processed every tick, with the next step's tick in `DepotState::due`;
  - every settling outcome is followed *D* ticks later;
  - the near recheck and the production hold are followed 1 tick later;
  - a working outcome is followed 1 tick later.
- The waiting gate uses the moving-destination test (Terms).
- The settling step empties the vehicle's order-level destination.
- `econ::power`: a disabling sets the flag, switches the hover off and makes the vehicle
  settled, in that order, after the guard reset. An enabling switches the hover on and ends
  being settled.

**S-0108 r60.**
- `Player::keep_stage(k)` replaces `continue_from`. A replacing start in `depot_anim` (sim) and
  in `mapview::slot_play` / `live.rs` (client) keeps the replaced animation's stage and takes its
  passes afresh.
- `slot_play::Slot::RESTART` gives the client's restart order for claim 12. The simulation's
  `DepotSlot` is declared in that order. The swap runs before the started slot's own creation.
- The interim mark `DepotState::sent_away` (Q-1620) is gone. After a send-away, the settling row
  acts only when *production* or *special-2* is running.

**S-0701 r25.**
- `speech::Line::text` reads the `Text` key, spelled exactly so; an empty value counts as absent.
- `Line::caption` returns the text, else the entry's name. `Audio::take_started` returns
  captions.
- `CAPTION_MS` (4 s) and `CAPTION_LINES` (3) now cite claim 26 instead of `nocite`.
