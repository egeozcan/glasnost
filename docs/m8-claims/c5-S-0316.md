# S-0316 revision 27 claim ledger, M8 step C5 part 1

S-0316 revision 27 (approved 2026-10-23, answering Q-1701; spec commit 3d90a2f names S-0316 as
its answer): electric-bolt shots, their sparks and how the bolt is drawn. No new quirk switch: the
spec names only `quirks.table_root_distance` (S-0204) and `quirks.chop_rounding` (S-0205) for the
bolt length, both existing. Voxel animations (Q-1702, C5 part 2) are out of scope.

**Simulation.** `combat::fire::shot` makes the bolt after the shot's line report when the shot put
its projectile on the map and the weapon has the electric-bolt flag without the laser flag
(`combat::effects::makes_bolt`, `combat::effects::bolt`). `bolt` reports `Effect::Bolt` (the
firer, weapon, slot, burst counter, the fire point of that slot and counter, the bolt end, a
building firer's position, whether the firer is a vehicle on the map) and then makes the spark
system (`emitters::electric_bolt_sparks`, the seam S-0616 left, now filled). With the bolt flag a
magnetic-beam weapon makes no wave. `Effect` is presentation only (not saved, not hashed); the
spark system is game state already covered by S-0616's save. No new hashed state: the save format
stays 70 and the AI game format 31. The bolt takes no scenario draw.

**Import.** `IsElectricBolt`, `IsAlternateColor`, `IsLaser`, `IsRadBeam` are new rows of
`sim_combat::WEAPON_COMBAT` (booleans, absent no; the coverage table lists them), read into
`WeaponRules::{electric_bolt, alt_colour, laser, rad_beam}`. The absent-`DefaultSparkSystem`
warning now names electric-bolt sparks (claim 6). The ruleset schema was regenerated.

**Drawing.** `app::mapview::bolts` (new): the bolt (`Bolt`), its length λ (`length`), levels
(`levels`, F1), the sway (`sway`, F2), the depth-first midpoint displacement with its draws
(`shape`, F3) and depth terms (F4), the view test (`line_in_view`), the colours from the general
palette's row 26 (`Colours`), and the pixels (`spots`, S-0514 claim 25's flash pixels).
`beams::Lines` keeps the bolts (`take_bolt`, attachment and following in `after_tick`,
`bolt_spots` newest first). `Live::draw_lines` pushes lasers, then bolts, then beam flashes; the
session hands the battlefield view to `Live::battlefield` before each drawing.

**Interim rules** (no request: the spec leaves these open or our engine's existing interims
cover them):
- One drawn frame per tick, as for the beam flash: a bolt's drawn frame *n* is its age in ticks;
  a redraw within one tick (a click while paused, a catch-up) draws frame *n* again with new
  draws.
- A piece's interior pixels are the nearest-integer 8-connected line of the beam flash, and the
  whole piece uses its leftmost end's unclipped bias (S-0316 Open questions, interior pixels;
  S-0514 Open questions).
- The bolt's draws are our view's cosmetic stream, as the beam spreads' (S-0518 claim 21's
  interim), not a stream shared with the sound choices; so claim 13's order between the phase
  draw and the report sound, and between bolt draws and S-0701 claim 30's per-instance sound
  draws, holds trivially (no draw of one stream can move the other).
- The view test uses the battlefield view at the redraw after the tick.

Tests: `crates/deterministic/sim/tests/claims_s0316.rs` (11, the play side, on invented types),
`crates/app/src/mapview/bolts_tests.rs` (18, the drawing on synthetic positions and an invented
palette), `glasnost-convert` `sim::combat_tests::electric_bolt_weapon_fields` and
`sim::gas_tests::default_spark_system_absent_warns`.
