# S-0213 r94 and S-0301 r67: claim ledger, M8 step A3 intake 70

S-0213 r90 to r94 (commit aa6a2fd, from 5dfbf5b) answers **Q-2247**, filed by
[intake 66](intake66-S-0213.md). S-0301 r64 to r67 (commit c2f4219, from 58a828f) answers
**Q-2246**, filed by the same intake. Both answers settle what intake 66 left as an ignored test
and a `design:` note. Requests filed: none.

## Built

- **The moment of a retarget's let-go** (S-0301 claim 5, *Moment of a retarget's let-go*, Q-2246).
  The fire place's retarget test is now made at the change of the firer's target, in whichever
  part of the tick makes the change, and never in the held system's turn. It reads nothing of the
  system's kind.
  - `emitters::retarget_let_go(w, owner)` no longer takes the system. It is called after every
    own-target write that holds the world: the order path (`orders::apply_behaviour`), the
    acquisition and override paths (`combat::acquire`), the obstacle attack (`drive`, `walk`),
    attack-move refreshes (`process`), the range cliff replacement (`combat::range`), deploys,
    strikes, carriers and aircraft attacks (`air`, `spawn`), slaves, parasites, linked units,
    transports' hand-down, and every revert of a suspended behaviour (`combat::held`,
    `teleport`, `cloak::machine`, `bridges`).
  - The clears that hold only the object (`spawn::set_own_target` with no target,
    `explicit_clear_own_target`, `redirected_to_none`, the plain clears in `combat::acquire` and
    `bridges::hut`) call `emitters::cleared`, which keeps the emitter and forgets its target
    (S-0301 Terms: a cleared target is not a new target).
  - New objects that start with a target and have no emitter yet (aircraft from a plane strike,
    strike spawns, deploy-made objects, survivors) make no test.
  - `particles::fire_turn` and `emitters::fire_place_let_go` no longer make the test.
    `fire_place_let_go` keeps the death let-go (r63). Intake 63's `design (Q-2246)` note is gone.
  - Since an attack order applies after the turn pass (S-0203 claim 7), the held system's turn in
    tick *t* + 1 is the first that the mark affects, for every kind.
- **A marked spark system's bursts** (S-0616 change (d)). No code change: `particles::spark_turn`
  never reads the mark, so a marked spark system bursts while its counter lasts. Now tested.
- **A vehicle in a copied *capture* or *sabotage*** (S-0213 claim 16b, *Joins*, Q-2247). No code
  change. The engine already follows S-0209 row VP28 and S-0207 claim 15 table T4:
  `drive::attack_obstacle` gives the vehicle *attack* on X by an override, with no destination,
  once its next step is a cell of X. Intake 66's ignored test is replaced by one that expects the
  override. A copied *sabotage* (run 2) is new and behaves the same.

## Tests changed

- `claims_s0616_emitters::claim_1_fire_emitter_let_go_events`: it wrote the firer's target
  directly and left the retarget test to `particles::fire_turn`. It now calls
  `emitters::retarget_let_go` at each write. Its "out of reach: let go" assertion moved from after
  the system's turn to the change itself. The within-reach case still runs the turn to check the
  system's target object.
- `claims_s0301_r63::fire_place_let_go_of_a_spark_kind_system`: the same change. It calls
  `retarget_let_go` after each write, in place of the spark system's turn in the far case.
- `claims_s0213_r89::claim_16b_a_vehicle_with_a_copied_capture_never_attacks_x` (ignored on
  Q-2247) is removed. `claim_16b_a_vehicle_with_a_copied_capture_attacks_x_by_the_override`
  replaces it, expecting the opposite at T's step into X, as r94 gives. The heads-for-X test now
  also checks that T is a drive vehicle with a primary weapon and is neither a train nor a crusher.
  It shares the run with the sabotage test (`part_ac`).
- The three new `claims_s0301_r67` tests that touch the moment fail on the engine before this
  intake: the at-change mark and u10's and the fire firer's particle in *t* + 1. The fourth, the
  spark bursts under the mark, passes on both, since `spark_turn` never read the mark.
