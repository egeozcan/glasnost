# S-0703 revision 34 claim ledger, M8 step C1 part 1

S-0703 revision 34 (approved 2026-10-23, a partial answer to Q-1700): which entries of the music
database can play in a skirmish, the next-track rule (repeat, list order, shuffle, the fallback)
and missing sound files. One new quirk switch, `quirks.music_fallback_entry_zero` (default on),
kept as `glasnost_audio::music::MusicQuirks::fallback_entry_zero` rather than in the rules'
`Quirks`, so that the simulation crates do not change; it has no configuration of its own yet.

**Simulation.** None. Music is presentation: it reads the local player's side at a game's start
and draws from the client's unsynchronised stream (S-0202 claim 11), never the scenario stream.
`git diff main -- crates/deterministic crates/sim-gate` is empty: the save format, the AI game
format and the gate goldens do not move; no arena or sweeps were run.

**Code.** `glasnost_fmt::wav` (WAVE header, IMA ADPCM, chunked decode, our own IMA writer and
fixtures), `glasnost_audio::music` (database, availability, next-track rule, player, music voice),
`glasnost_audio::music_files` (the install's music database, settings file and track files),
`Vfs::loose_only` (the settings file), the mixer's music voice and volume,
`glasnost_app::game::install` and `client` (the music of each skirmish), and
`glasnost_app::game::music_share` (the web's OPFS copy; our own design).

**Interim rules**, each with a request. All four are answered by S-0703 revision 51 and were
retired at M8 step A3 intake 12 ([intake12-S-0703-S-0701.md](intake12-S-0703-S-0701.md)); the
list below and the rows that name them record this step as built:
- Q-1830: music starts when the skirmish's audio starts (`Audio::start`); the first update picks
  with no ended track; each game forgets the last one's ended track.
- Q-1831: the shuffle and repeat settings are off when the settings file or its key is missing or
  malformed; they are read when the install is read; there are no in-game controls.
- Q-1832: a file that is found counts as present whatever it holds; one that does not parse as a
  WAVE file fails its start like one that cannot be opened; truncated data ends where the data
  ends (its frames counted from the data present); a name that already ends in `.wav` gets the
  extension again.
- Q-1833: music plays only while the game is played (the options menu open or not, picks
  included) and stops when play ends or the game is left. On our own settings screen opened from
  the game the audio is not serviced, as for sound: the track sounds to its end and the next pick
  waits for the return.

Our own UI (no claim): the music volume of our settings scales the music voice linearly, music
skipping the master level and the sound option (S-0703 leaves the scaling open, Q-1700 question 5).
No next, previous or stop control was built.

Tests: `crates/audio/src/music_tests.rs` (35, engine fixtures of MU1 to MU7 on synthetic databases
and tones), `crates/audio/src/music_files.rs` (1), `crates/fmt/src/wav.rs` (6),
`crates/app/src/game/music_share.rs` (5), and the lane `crates/app/tests/c1_lane.rs` (ignored,
real install).
