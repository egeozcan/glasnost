## Need
S-0607 F3 says "Negative wrapped sums are outside this spec's established domain". The engine must decide anyway. This needs a horizontal separation of more than about 46 340 leptons, which an ordinary weapon range does not reach.

## Questions
1. When the 32-bit sum wraps negative, is a wave created?

## Current workaround
No wave is created. The shot is fired as usual.
