## Need
S-0606 claim 6 describes the stages while the entering vehicle obeys them; it does not say what happens if the player orders the vehicle elsewhere after contact (during clearing, turning in, or on the entry track).

## Questions
1. Does a move order during turning in or driving in break off the entry (vehicle leaves, bunker goes back to clearing)?
2. Does an order given while the vehicle is on the entry track take effect at once or only after the track ends?

## Possible observation
Order the vehicle away at each entry stage and note where it ends and what the bunker does next.

## Current workaround
Interim rule: the order's ordinary contact break (S-0213 table T3 (b)) ends the contact; the bunker's next repair processing finds no vehicle to take in and goes back to clearing with guard queued (table S1's no-vehicle row). A track in progress finishes first under the ordinary drive rules.
