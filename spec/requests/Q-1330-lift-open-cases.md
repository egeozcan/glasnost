## Need
Several small cases that S-0607 revision 2 leaves open, each needing a rule in the engine.

## Questions
1. Claims 3 and 4: is a resource-gathering vehicle on a temporary locomotor tested against claim 3's conditions (size, factory, layer) before claim 4's special case, or does claim 4 apply first?
2. Passengers: does a dropped transport's impact (claim 18) kill its passengers, let them escape, or remove them with it? Claim 13 says they stay in at the drop.
3. Does a second destruction credit (S-0607 Open questions, a first-detonation kill) count for the result screen?
4. Can a seized unit deploy, or unload passengers, while seized, or while set down before restoration?
5. Claim 21: does a wave's target entering a transport, bunker or building ("off the map" but not destroyed) count as leaving play?

## Current workaround
1. Claim 3 first, then claim 4.
2. Passengers go with the transport under the ordinary destruction rules of the impact.
3. Only the ordinary credit of each destruction.
4. No rule added: deploy and unload orders go through the ordinary checks.
5. Only removal from the game ends the wave. A target that goes inside changes the firer's target, which ends the wave anyway.
