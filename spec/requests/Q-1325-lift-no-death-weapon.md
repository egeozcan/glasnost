## Need
S-0607 claim 18 produces two detonations "using the projectile of the game-wide death weapon". Data can leave that weapon absent.

## Questions
1. With no game-wide death weapon, do the two detonations still happen, with the same warheads and amount?
2. Or does nothing happen, so that the unit is then removed (or set down with the survivor quirk off) without damage?

## Current workaround
Each detonation becomes a plain area detonation of its warhead at its point, with the same amount, source and house.
