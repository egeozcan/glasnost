## Need

M-0021's part B row gives the G vehicles dead per run: 4, 4, 7 with F on side A and 7, 6, 7 with F
on side B (seeds 1000 to 1002), every F vehicle dead. In our runs the count does not change with
the seed within a side (4, 4, 4 and 7, 7, 7), although our seeds do differ: they first differ at a
G vehicle's second shot (the record's own first difference), the first hit that differs falls in
that same tick, and the first death differs between the seeds in its tick. So ours splits where
the record says it does, and the counts still differ from the record's in 2 of 6 runs.

The record says part B's deaths "differ between the seeds later, after further shots have moved
apart", and its Notes say the recordings do not show whether other seed-dependent draws or choices
act later. Nothing in the record says which later choice or draw sets the count of G vehicles
that die, so which claim of ours differs from the original there cannot be named from the record.

## Questions

1. For each part B run, in which order do the vehicles die, as the side and the place in the
   creation order of each (no ticks)?
2. For each side, the first death at which the three seeds' orders differ (the first, second, and
   so on in the order of deaths), and whether it is an F or a G vehicle?
3. In each part B run, does the target of any shot, after the first death, differ between the
   seeds (the first such shot: its firer's side and its place in the creation order)?

## Possible observation

Questions 1 and 2 are read from the shots, hits and per-tick rows of any part B run; question 3 from
the shot rows of the three seeds.

## Current workaround

None in the simulation: the row stays failing and the harness reports ours beside the record's.
