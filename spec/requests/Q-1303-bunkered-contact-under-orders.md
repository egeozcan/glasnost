## Need
S-0606 claim 7 says the bunkered vehicle stays the bunker's contact. S-0213 lets most orders, and the deploy order, break contact off. The spec does not say which wins for a bunkered vehicle.

## Questions
1. After an attack order or a deploy order to a bunkered vehicle, is it still the bunker's contact (for example, can a second vehicle now be given the bunker as destination before the first leaves)?

## Possible observation
Give a bunkered vehicle an attack order, then send a second vehicle to the bunker.

## Current workaround
Interim rule: a bunkered vehicle keeps its contact with its bunker under orders, including the deploy order.
