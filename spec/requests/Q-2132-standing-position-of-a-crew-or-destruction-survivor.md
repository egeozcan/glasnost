## Need
S-0312 claim 13 puts a destroyed vehicle's crew, and claim 18 a destroyed building's survivor,
"on a free standing position" of its cell, and neither unit is placed when no position is free.
The claims give the draw order (type, creation, health, then the scatter request) and no draw
for the position. They do not say which free position is taken, nor what makes one free.

The neighbouring rules differ: an absorbed passenger that comes out (claim 24) and a sale
survivor (S-0408 claim 7) use S-0208's centre-class choice, one draw over 0 to 3 and then the
first free position of row R*k* of table O, and a cell refused by table O's first row takes no
draw and gives no position. Claim 18 also says that the destroyed building still occupies its
foundation cells during the first round, so the building's own presence may matter.

## Questions
1. Which free position do a crew (claim 13) and a destruction survivor (claim 18) take: the
   first free one in some fixed order (which order), or a choice like S-0208's centre class (and
   then with which draw, and where in the draw order)?
2. What makes a position free for them: only the cell's infantry positions, or also the
   vehicle-present and object-present flags of S-0208 table O's first row? Does the destroyed
   building in its own foundation cells (claim 18's first round) block them?
3. Is the answer the same for claim 21's second round, after the building has left its cells?

## Possible observation
A crewed test building (S-0312 scenario D13) with two of a foundation cell's positions taken by
own infantry, destroyed many times with the survivor chance forced: record each survivor's
position and the draw count. Likewise for a crewed vehicle (scenario D12) in a cell with some
positions taken.

## Current workaround
The first free position in S-0208 table P order (0 to 4), reading only the cell's infantry
positions on the ground layer; no draw. A cell with all five taken places no one (the creation
draw is taken, no health draw, the unit does not count against *C*). This keeps the draw order
that claims 13 and 18 give.
