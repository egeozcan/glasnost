## Need
S-0607 claim 11 places the lifter's *rearming* at S-0301 claim 9's rank 4 and says its precedence against other rank-4 answers is not specified. One case matters for play. A lifter that fires again at the unit it already holds (claim 2: the hit does nothing) sees a target that is now in the air. The air and projectile tests could then answer *illegal*, which drops the target and so falls back to rest, and that ends the grip (claim 12).

## Questions
1. While a lifter grips a unit and keeps that unit as its target, which answer does a check against that unit give: *rearming*, *illegal* (the unit is airborne and the beam cannot hit air), or an ordinary *fire*?
2. Does the lifter keep that target for the whole pull?

## Possible observation
Scenario L2 or L9: record the lifter's target and behaviour during a pull of about 6 cells.

## Current workaround
A magnetic-beam weapon against the unit its firer grips answers *rearming*, ahead of the projectile and air tests. So the lifter keeps its target and the grip lasts until arrival. Claim 11's own *rearming* (other non-building targets while the wave lives) follows the beam test.
