## Need
S-0207 claim 27 names a destination refusal and leaves its cases to other specs. S-0606 says the bunkered vehicle is not movable and stays at the reference point, but does not say what happens when a destination is assigned to it anyway (a script, a team, a scatter).

## Questions
1. Does a bunkered vehicle that is given a destination by any route stay in the bunker?

## Current workaround
Interim rule: a non-empty destination is refused while the bunker link holds, and one written by another route is cleared at the start of the vehicle's behaviour turn.
