## Need
S-0513 revision 43, claim 39, and its Changes for S-0301 add a rank-4 reason to the firing check:
a target in the leaving stage of a superweapon warp answers *illegal* unless the asking weapon is a
temporal weapon. The same Changes entry says: "Whether a target held by a temporal beam gets the
same answer is not settled here."

A warp member can be held by a temporal beam during its leaving stage: its being-erased state
ends only at departure (claim 39, table T2). So our firing check must give some answer when any
weapon asks about such a member. S-0301 and S-0514 give the firer that already holds this very
target with a temporal weapon an earlier *rearming* (S-0514 claim 19), and that answer is ranked
above the new reason, so it is not in question. The question is about every other asker.

## Questions
1. A warp member in the leaving stage is held by a temporal beam of some firer *F*. Another firer
   asks the firing check about the member with a weapon that is not a temporal weapon. Is the
   answer *illegal* (the leaving-stage reason), or does the temporal hold change it, and to what?
2. The same, when the asking weapon is a temporal weapon of a firer other than *F*: does the
   leaving-stage exception for temporal weapons apply, so that the rest of the check decides, or
   does the hold give its own answer?
3. Does it matter whether *F*'s beam started before the launch or during the leaving stage?

## Possible observation
S-0513 SW12 part (i)'s set-up with one extra firer that holds the member with a temporal beam from
before the launch, and two more askers near it, one with a temporal weapon and one without. Record
the askers' targets and whether they fire in the leaving stage.

## Current workaround
The leaving-stage reason applies whatever holds the member: a weapon that is not a temporal weapon
gets *illegal*, and a temporal weapon passes it and the rest of the firing check decides (S-0301
claim 9). The held-temporal-target *rearming* of S-0514 claim 19 keeps its earlier rank for *F*
itself.
